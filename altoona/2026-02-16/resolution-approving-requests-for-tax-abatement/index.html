      Resolution approving Requests for Tax Abatement

      Altoona City Council · Feb 16, 2026 · Taxes and fees

      Recorded in the minutes as “Resolution approving Requests for Tax Abatement”. Passed 4–0 as part of the consent agenda; Yes: Duer, McFadden, Mertz, Stewart.

      What this means (Abatement): In city government this usually means one of two things: reducing property taxes on new construction for a set number of years, or requiring or arranging the cleanup of a nuisance or unsafe property.
