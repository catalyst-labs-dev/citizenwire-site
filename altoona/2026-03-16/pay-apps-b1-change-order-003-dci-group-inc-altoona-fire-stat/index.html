      What this means
      Change order
      An approved change to a construction contract the city already signed. It can raise or lower the price, or change the work or the schedule.
      Pay application
      A contractor’s request to be paid for work finished so far on an approved project. Cities often hold back a share, called retainage, until the job is complete.

      A general definition of this kind of action, not a statement about this item.
