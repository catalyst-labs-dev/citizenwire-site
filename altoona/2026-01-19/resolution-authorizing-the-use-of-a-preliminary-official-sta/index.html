      Altoona City Council · Jan 19, 2026 · Budget

      Recorded in the minutes as “6. Resolution authorizing the use of a preliminary official statement in connection to the sale of General Obligation Urban Renewal Refunding Bonds, Series 2026A and authorizing certain parameters for the acceptance and…”. Passed 5–0; Yes: Duer, Evans, McFadden, Mertz, Stewart. Amount in the record: $1,300,000.

      What this means (Tax increment financing (TIF)): Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “6. Resolution authorizing the use of a preliminary official statement in connection to the sale of General Obligation Urban Renewal Refunding Bonds, Series 2026A and authorizing certain parameters for the acceptance and approval of a Bond Purchase Agreement. Motion: Moved by Duer”. The minutes add: “Finance Director Andy Lent stated Council has approved staff moving forward with the refinancing of the 2016C Bond Series to obtain a better interest rate that will approximately save $1.3 Million over ten years. As part of the bond issue process a Preliminary Official Statement must be approved by Council. The new debt service will be known as the 2026A Series.”

      Motion as recorded: “Moved by Duer”.
