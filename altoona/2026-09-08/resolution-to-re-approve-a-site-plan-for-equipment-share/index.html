      Resolution to re-approve a site plan for Equipment Share

      • Altoona City Council
      • Sep 8, 2026
      • Zoning
      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “9. Resolution to re-approve a site plan for Equipment Share. Motion: Moved by Dunwoody”. The minutes add: “Community Development Director John Shaw stated this resolution is to re-approve a Site Plan for Equipment Share. The plan proposes the construction of an 11,869 sq ft building generally located in our northwest industrial area. Councilmember Dunwoody motioned approval subject to deficiencies being met.” Why this has a full page: zoning.

      Background, staff memo and alternatives: Not in the minutes. See the Agenda packet: Not in the record.

      The decision

      Passed 4–0. Motion as recorded: “Moved by Dunwoody”. Minutes (saved copy)

      What this means
      Site plan
      A detailed drawing of how one property will be built, such as buildings, parking, driveways and landscaping. The city checks it against its rules.
      Resolution
      A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
