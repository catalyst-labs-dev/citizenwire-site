      Stubbs Engineering, 1609 N Ankeny Blvd STE 230

      Altoona City Council · Jan 5, 2026 · Zoning

      Recorded in the minutes as “2. Stubbs Engineering, 1609 N Ankeny Blvd STE 230, Ankeny stated Stubbs Development is proposing a final plat that lays out a single-family development on 7.56 acres of land. Motion: Moved by Duer”. Passed 3–0; Yes: Duer, McFadden, Stewart. Abstained: Evans.
