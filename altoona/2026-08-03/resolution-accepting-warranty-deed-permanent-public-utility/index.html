      Resolution accepting Warranty Deed, Permanent Public Utility Easement

      Altoona City Council · Aug 3, 2026 · City government

      Recorded in the minutes as “Resolution accepting Warranty Deed, Permanent Public Utility Easement, Ingress/Egress Easement, Permanent Water Main Easement, Permanent Storm Sewer Easement, and Temporary Construction Easement – Signature Ernst Land Development LLC - $166,400…”. Passed 3–0 as part of the consent agenda; Yes: Duer, Evans, Stewart. Amount in the record: $166,400.

      What this means (Easement): A right for the city or a utility to use part of someone’s land for a set purpose, such as pipes or access.
