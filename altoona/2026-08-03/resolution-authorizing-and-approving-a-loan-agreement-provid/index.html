      Resolution authorizing and approving a Loan Agreement, providing for issuance of $7,150,000…

      Altoona City Council · Aug 3, 2026 · Taxes and fees · Budget

      Recorded in the minutes as “5. Resolution authorizing and approving a Loan Agreement, providing for issuance of $7,150,000 General Obligation Urban Renewal Bonds, Series 2026E and providing for the levy of taxes to pay the same. Motion: Moved by…”. Passed 3–0; Yes: Duer, Evans, Stewart. Amount in the record: $7,150,000.

      What this means (Tax increment financing (TIF)): Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.
