      How to credit

      Data: Citizen Wire (citizenwire.org), CC BY 4.0

      The license covers Citizen Wire's compiled data. The minutes and other source documents are public records of the City of Des Moines.

      Reading the data

      • "Not in the record" means the minutes do not say. It is never used to mean absent.
      • A member is listed as absent only when the minutes list them as absent.
      • Names of members of the public are replaced with a neutral description, such as "a speaker".
      • IDs do not change once published. Earlier IDs are listed in the id_crosswalk table.
