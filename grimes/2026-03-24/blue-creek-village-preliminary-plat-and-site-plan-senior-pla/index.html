      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “3. Blue Creek Village Preliminary Plat and Site Plan Senior Planner Coffey shares the details of the proposed plat and site plan located just west of the Oakview Middle School. Motion: approve Blue Creek Village Preliminary Plat and Site Plan”. The minutes add: “Zoned for medium-density residential, the site plan includes 164 ranch- style townhomes with two to four units per building. Mayor Gisch asked for approval of Blue Creek Village Preliminary Plat and Site Plan”

      Motion as recorded: “approve Blue Creek Village Preliminary Plat and Site Plan”.

      The minutes give only the count (5–0), so individual votes are Not in the record. Vote line as printed: “Roll Call: Ayes: All. Nays: None.”.
