      First Reading of Ordinance 809 Brookside Village

      Recorded in the minutes as “7. First Reading of Ordinance 809 Brookside Village North Rezone Senior Planner Coffey gave an overview of the proposed rezone with a conceptual plan including 8 acres of medium-density residential and 6 acres of…”. Passed 5–0; names not recorded.

      What this means (Ordinance readings): An ordinance is a local law.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “7. First Reading of Ordinance 809 Brookside Village North Rezone Senior Planner Coffey gave an overview of the proposed rezone with a conceptual plan including 8 acres of medium-density residential and 6 acres of high-density residential and a fee in lieu of the parkland dedication. Motion: approve First Reading of Ordinance 809 Brookside Village North Rezone”. The minutes add: “Council members asked questions about the park that the fee in lieu would be contributed to for park improvements. Mayor Gisch asked for approval of First Reading of Ordinance 809 Brookside Village North Rezone”

      Motion as recorded: “approve First Reading of Ordinance 809 Brookside Village North Rezone”.

      The minutes give only the count (5–0), so individual votes are Not in the record. Vote line as printed: “Roll Call: Ayes: All. Nays: None.”.
