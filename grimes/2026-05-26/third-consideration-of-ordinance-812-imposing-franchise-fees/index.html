      What this means
      Ordinance readings
      An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.
      Franchise fee
      A fee a utility pays for using city streets and land for its lines. It is usually charged to customers as a percentage of their bills.
