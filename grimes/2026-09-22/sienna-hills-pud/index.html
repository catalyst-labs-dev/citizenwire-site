      Sienna Hills PUD

      Grimes City Council · Sep 22, 2026 · Zoning

      Recorded in the minutes as “1. Ordinance 817 Sienna Hills PUD Amendment Development Services Director Pfaltzgraff presented details of the proposed amendment including a portion of the residential lots moving from seven-foot side yard setbacks to…”. Passed 5–0; names not recorded.

      What this means (Planned unit development (PUD)): A custom zoning district with rules written for one project, instead of the standard district rules.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Ordinance 817 Sienna Hills PUD Amendment Development Services Director Pfaltzgraff presented details of the proposed amendment including a portion of the residential lots moving from seven-foot side yard setbacks to five-foot. Motion: approve the Second Consideration of Ordinance 817 Sienna Hills PUD Amendment”. The minutes add: “Mayor and Council asked questions and discussed the proposal with staff and the developer. Mayor Gisch asked for approval of the Second Consideration of Ordinance 817 Sienna Hills PUD Amendment”

      Motion as recorded: “approve the Second Consideration of Ordinance 817 Sienna Hills PUD Amendment”.

      The minutes give only the count (5–0), so individual votes are Not in the record. Vote line as printed: “Roll Call: Ayes: All. Nays: None.”.
