Directors met in a regular session from 5:30?5:50pm. Board Chair Martorano presiding.
Present: Alonzo, Campos, Dix, Hopkins, Howell, Mayberry-Mayes (virtual), Martorano
The directors approved the following items: 7-0
1. Approval of agenda
The directors held a public forum for speakers attending in person.
Interim Superintendent Smith recognized the Central Academy Potters and their instructor Dara Green. This year the pottery students were selected to create the Iowa State Fair's limited edition Blue Ribbon Foundation pottery.
Each piece is made by students and sold exclusively at the Iowa State Fair with all proceeds directly supporting the pottery program.
