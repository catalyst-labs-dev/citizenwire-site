Items of privilege:
Chairs Report presented by board members. Board member Campos shared that Harding Middle school is celebrating their 100 year anniversary and invited the public to join in on their beautification project September 18th at 8am.
Board member Hopkins gave a shoutout to all the summer coaches. Dr. Skylar Mayberry-Mayes thanked the community partners for their work as the start of the school year kicks off, also shared his gratitude to teachers. Board chair Martorano thanked the community for attending the community engagement sessions about the boundary proposals. Interim Superintendent Smith shared his gratitude for CFUM and the Reichardt Community Recreation Center for their continued support.  He also acknowledged and accepted responsibility for the teacher shift at the start of the school year.
Without objection the directors adjourned at 6:08pm. The board took a brief break then transitioned into room 2002 for a work session on board governance and monitoring led by the data team. Following the work session, the board moved into a private exempt session pursuant to Iowa Code section 21.9 to discuss strategy in matters relating to employment conditions of employees not covered by a collective bargaining agreement.
