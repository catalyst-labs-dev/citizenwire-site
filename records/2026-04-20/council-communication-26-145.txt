contract documents, engineer’s estimate, receive and file bids, and designating lowest responsible

SYNOPSIS:

This is a public hearing to allow for public input on the Birdland Park Phase 1B Improvements and
take the necessary actions as required by Iowa Code to authorize a contract for construction of the
proposed project. The Birdland Park Phase 1B project is the second of three (3) sub-phase projects
that make up Phase 1 of the community and stakeholder driven Birdland Park and Marina Master Plan.
This project includes a new Portland Concrete Cement (PCC) parking lot, open green space, new
playground area, skate ribbon, restroom and concessions building, sidewalks and trail along with other
amenities. The project also includes native and diverse plantings, including prairie and savanna
vegetation to broadly support water quality and wildlife habitat.

  •   A master plan for Birdland Park and Marina was developed in 2021 and was approved by the
      Parks and Recreation Board and adopted by the City Council. The master plan outlines a
      logical, highest value approach to implementing multiple phases of proposed improvements. In
      total the project was estimated to cost $54.4 million in 2021, and implementation is planned to
      spread across five (5) recommended phases.

  •   Informed by LiveDSM, the Parks and Recreation Department’s comprehensive plan, the
      Birdland master planning project has been community and stakeholder driven. To facilitate
      project feedback and achieve community support from the neighborhood and everyday park
      users, three (3) public meetings were held at different phases of the project’s development. The
      meetings garnered over 200 attendees and generated 350 plus follow-up survey responses.
      Additional public engagement for Phase 1 projects was ongoing throughout 2024 and 2025 with
      all master plan stakeholders and in-person presentations to Union Park, River Bend, Highland
      Park/Oak Park, and Capitol Park Neighborhood Associations. The overarching sentiment was
      appreciation that the approved and adopted master plan is being implemented and realized.

•   Given the complexity and scale of the master plan, intentional sequencing or phasing is
    recommended to support improvements functioning on their own and are not contingent upon a
    subsequent phase.

•   Birdland Park and Marina Master Plan – Phase 1B Project Improvements Summary:
       o Rehabilitated and right-sized parking lot with lighting, stormwater management biocell
         with native plantings, and one-way circulation for improved pedestrian safety.
       o Great lawn community gathering space with boardwalk overlook.
       o Destination quality, nature themed playground with unitary tile surfacing to support
         enhanced Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) accessibility.
       o Multi-functional skating ribbon to support year-round skating usage and seasonal
         Traffic Safety Garden programming where park users can learn to safely navigate the
         urban environment on a bicycle.
       o Flood-proofed restroom and concessions building to be open year-round.
       o Improvements to the Birdland Park Trail spur off the Neal Smith Trail.
       o Lagoon overlook deck on the Ding Darling Enclosed Shelter to expand capacity for the
         most popular rental shelter in the park system.
       o Multiple plazas, seating areas, and other park user amenities to best support longer
         visits and the diverse recreational offerings in the park more broadly.
       o Multiple naturalized drainage areas and diverse native plantings through the park.

•   The Birdland Phase 1B project leverages approximately $5.8 million in external funding from
    various local, state, and federal sources as listed in the Fiscal Impact Statement.

•   A dedicated project webpage offers a repository of project information including links to digital
    public meetings, video recordings from prior meetings, downloadable exhibits such as the
    presentations slides from public meetings and the master plan concept, and updates on the plan
    adoption process. The project webpage can be found on the City’s website under Parks and
    Recreation Department by navigating to current planning projects.

•   In late 2022, a professional service agreement was completed with SmithGroup, Inc. to provide
    engineering design for two sites within Birdland Park, Phase 1A and Phase 1B.

•   This Phase 1B project is anticipated to begin in spring 2026 and be completed in summer 2027.
