          RESOLUTION APPROVING PRELIMINARY TERMS OF AN URBAN RENEWAL
       DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT WITH INGERSOLL PROPERTIES GROUP, LLC FOR THE
      REHABILITATION OF 32,437 SQUARE FOOT COMMERCIAL PROPERTY AND ADJACENT
                    PARKING FACILITIES AT 2700 INGERSOLL AVENUE

             WHEREAS, Ingersoll Properties Group, LLC ("Developer"), represented by Cb-istensen
      Development, proposes to undertake the rehabilitation of the existing commercial building and adjacent
     parking lot located at 2700 Ingersoll Avenue for a Program of All-inclusive Care for the Elderly ("PACE"),
      (collectively "Improvements") in the Ingersoll-Grand Urban Renewal Area; and


             WHEREAS, construction of the Improvements is anticipated to be completed in 2027 at an estimated
     total project cost of $8,200,00.00, subject to receipt of the financial assistance identified below; and

             WHEREAS, the Developer seeks reimbursement from the taxable building valuations
     (exclusive of land) at a capped maximum value of $850,000 on a net present basis based upon
     payment of 80% TIP in years one (1) through eight (8); and

             WHEREAS, the Developer has provided the Office of Economic Development with a
     financial analysis of the project, indicating a gap between actual project costs and the project
     appraisal and seeks this financial assistance package to be provided from project generated tax
     increment as more specifically described in the accompanying Council Communication.


             NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the City Council of the City ofDes
     Moines, Iowa, as follows:


                  1. The City Council hereby approves the preliminary terms of agreement as set forth
                 in the accompanying Council Communication; and,


                 2. Directs the City Manager or his designee to negotiate final terms of agreement for
                 future consideration by the City Council.



                                         (Council Communication No. 26-212)



     MOVED BY TO ADOPT.
