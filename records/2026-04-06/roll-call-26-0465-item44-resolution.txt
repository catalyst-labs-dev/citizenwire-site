        Guthrie Avenue Bridge Rehabilitation over the Union Pacific Railroad, Activity ID 122020001
        IDOT Project No. STP-U-1945(859)--SG-77

        The improvement includes structural repairs and reconfiguring the bridge cross-section to accommodate a
        10-foot-wide multi-use sidepath along the south side of the bridge. The necessary bridge work to
        accommodate the sidepath includes containment and coordination for working over railroad right-of-way,
        removal of bridge barrier rails, removal and replacement of bridge expansion joints, steel bearing repairs,
        bridge painting of bearings and beams, removing and replacing deck sections, reinforcing steel and
        structural concrete, and construction of new sidewalks, railings and fences. The reconfigured bridge cross-
        section will require the approach roadway pavement to be removed and reconstructed, including new
        concrete pavement, new traffic signalization at Delaware Avenue, new sidewalks, traffic control and traffic
        staging, temporary traffic signals, temporary pavement, pavement markings, and erosion control, all in
        accordance with the contract documents including Plan File No. 678-225/373, located on Guthrie Avenue
        over the Union Pacific Railroad, west of Delaware Avenue, Des Moines, Iowa; and
