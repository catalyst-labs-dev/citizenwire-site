AGENDA HEADING:

Hearing on Guthrie Avenue Bridge Rehabilitation over the Union Pacific Railroad: Resolution
approving plans, specifications, form of contract documents, engineer’s estimate, receive and file bids,
and designating lowest responsible bidder as Jasper Construction Services, Inc.: (Cliff Rhoads,
President), $6,124,564.01.


SYNOPSIS:

This is a public hearing to allow for public input on the Guthrie Avenue Bridge Rehabilitation over the
Union Pacific Railroad and take the necessary actions as required by Iowa Code to authorize a contract
for construction of the proposed project. This project includes rehabilitation of the Guthrie Avenue
Bridge over Union Pacific Railroad, including the following major items of work:

                                  •   Removal and replacement of the concrete bridge deck overlay.
                                  •   Replacement of bridge expansion joints.
                                  •   Repainting structural steel.
                                  •   Replacement of the concrete barrier walls and medians along the bridge.
                                  •   Isolated structural repairs to the bridge deck.
                                  •   Modifying the cross section to add a 10-foot-wide multi-use sidepath along the south side of the
                                      bridge.
                                  •   Installation of structural steel pedestrian handrailing and chain link fencing along the bridge.

This project also includes intersection improvements at Guthrie Avenue and Delaware Avenue,
including reconstruction of the intersection pavement and replacement of the traffic signals. The
project will be completed in several construction stages to maintain traffic in each direction.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

  •   On March 9, 2026, under Roll Call No. 26-0286, the City Council received and filed plans,
      specifications, form of contract documents, and City Engineer's estimate for the construction of
      the Guthrie Avenue Bridge Rehabilitation over the Union Pacific Railroad. Before the City
      Council can enter into a contract, it must hold a public hearing and approve the proposed plans,
      specifications, and form of contract documents and engineer's estimate for the construction of
      the Guthrie Avenue Bridge Rehabilitation over the Union Pacific Railroad, Activity ID 12-
      2020-001. The improvement includes structural repairs and reconfiguring the bridge cross-
      section to accommodate a 10-foot-wide multi-use sidepath along the south side of the bridge.
      The necessary bridge work to accommodate the sidepath includes containment and coordination
      for working over railroad right-of-way, removal of bridge barrier rails, removal and replacement
      of bridge expansion joints, steel bearing repairs, bridge painting of bearings and beams,
      removing and replacing deck sections, reinforcing steel and structural concrete, and construction
      of new sidewalks, railings and fences. The reconfigured bridge cross-section will require the
      approach roadway pavement to be removed and reconstructed, including new concrete
      pavement, new traffic signalization at Delaware Avenue, new sidewalks, traffic control and
      traffic staging, temporary traffic signals, temporary pavement, pavement markings, and erosion
      control, all in accordance with the contract documents including Plan File No. 678-225/373,
      located on Guthrie Avenue over the Union Pacific Railroad, west of Delaware Avenue.

  •   Sealed bids for said improvement were submitted by the following bidders:
         o Jasper Construction Services, Inc., Newton, IA - Low Bid
         o Cramer and Associates, Inc., Grimes, IA

  •   The bids, in accordance with a previously approved Iowa DOT Agreement, were received and
      opened by the Iowa DOT on March 17, 2026.

  •   The City Engineer has determined that the lowest responsive, responsible bid for the
      construction of said improvement was submitted by Jasper Construction Services, Inc. 928 N
      19th Avenue East, Newton, IA, 50208; (Cliff Rhoads, President), in the amount of
      $6,124,564.01, and said bid should be accepted. The Finance Director has determined that
      funds are available for said improvement as stated above.

  •   The Guthrie Avenue bridge over the Union Pacific Railroad was constructed in 1978. In 2015,
      this bridge was listed as a high priority repair and a targeted joint replacement and strengthening
      project was completed, however a more extensive rehabilitation project was postponed. The
      2019 Bridge Inspection Report continues to list the condition of the bridge as “Fair/Poor” with
      chloride contaminated concrete of the deck and sidewalks, and the concrete barrier rails nearing
      the end of useful life.

  •   The traffic signals at Guthrie Avenue and Delaware Avenue were constructed in 1978 and need
      replacement.

  •   The City also has been working on a plan to construct a PCC multi-use sidepath on the south
      side of Guthrie Avenue from Glenbrook Drive to Hubbell Avenue in three (3) phases:
      o Phase 1 – Guthrie Avenue from East 21st Street to Hubbell Avenue was completed in the
        fall of 2024.
