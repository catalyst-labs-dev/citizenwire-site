General Election - November 3, 2026 - Voter Information

Published 7/16/2026 08:46:47 AM

The general election is held in even-numbered years to elect federal, state, county and township officers. Constitutional amendments and public measures for cities and counties may also be on the ballot.

Voter Information

Key Dates:

  • First day to vote early at the election office: October 14
    • The office will be open during our regular office hours.
    • The office will also be open on Saturday, October 24 (8:00am - 4:00pm) and October 31 (8:00am - 5:00pm)
  • Last day to submit Absentee Ballot Requests: October 19
    • You can no longer request a ballot to be mailed to you.
  • Pre-Registration Deadline: October 19
    • Any voter registration forms submitted after this date will not take effect until after Election Day.
    • Voters can still register to vote or make changes when they vote early in-person.
    • Voters can register to vote or make changes on Election Day.
  • Election Day: November 3
    • Polls will be open from 7:00am - 8:00pm
  • Absentee ballots must be received by November 3 @ 8:00pm.

Public Notices

Public notices will be posted here as they are published.

All satellites will be open from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM
Each satellite will be provided ballot styles for the area. Ballot-marking devices will be available for voters requiring assistance and for voters that reside anywhere in Polk County.
