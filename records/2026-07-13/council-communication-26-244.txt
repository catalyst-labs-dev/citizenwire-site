AGENDA HEADING:

First consideration to amend Chapter 26 of the Municipal Code – Buildings and Building Regulations


SYNOPSIS:

Approval of this action amends Chapter 26 of the Municipal Code with following actions:
                                   •   Requires a re-roofing permit for both commercial and residential structures beginning on
                                       January 1, 2027.
                                   •   Modifies the electrical code to comply with recent legislative changes to the State Electrical
                                       Code.


FISCAL IMPACT:

Amount: Estimated at $150,000

Funding Source: Permit fees from re-roofing projects for residential and commercial structures.


ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

                                   •   The second and third reading is requested to be waived due to the editorial nature of these code
                                       changes.

                                   •   The City of Des Moines Building and Fire Code Board of Appeals has reviewed and
                                       recommends adoption of these changes to Chapter 26.
