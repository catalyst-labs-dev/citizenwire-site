Re: FY 2027 Compensation for City Council Appointees




     Mayor Boesen, Councilmember Simonson, and Councilmember Voss:


     As directed by the Personnel Committee, base annual salaries shall increase 3% from fiscal
     year 2026 for the City Manager, City Attorney and City Clerk, which is consistent with the
     across-the-board pay increase provided to other Supervisor, Professional and Management
     employees, if approved and adopted by the Mayor and City Council on Monday, July 13,
     2026.

     Base annual salaries for the City Council appointees effective June 22, 2026 are:

        • City Manager Scott Sanders - $337,563

        • City Attorney Jeff Lester - $264,472

        • City Clerk Laura Baumgartner - $ 151,528

     The retention bonus for the City Manager and City Attorney shall continue in fiscal year
     2027 at 6.5% under the same provisions previously approved by Roll Call 20-1492.

     The City Clerk may elect to convert up to one week of accrued vacation leave to a payment
     equal in value for such vacation time in the fiscal year.

     All other compensation and benefits shall continue, and health and dental benefit premium
     contributions shall match those of the City Directors.
