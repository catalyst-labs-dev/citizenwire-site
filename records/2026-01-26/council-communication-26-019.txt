AGENDA HEADING:

Hearing on East Court Avenue Reconstruction from East 7th Street to East 14th Street: Resolution
approving plans, specifications, form of contract documents, engineer’s estimate, receive and file bids,
and designating lowest responsible bidder as Reilly Construction Co., Inc.: (Chris R. Reilly, President),
$4,198,776.71.


SYNOPSIS:

This is a public hearing to allow for public input on the East Court Avenue Reconstruction from East
7th Street to East 14th Street and take the necessary actions as required by Iowa Code to authorize a
contract for construction of the proposed project. This project provides for full reconstruction of East
Court Avenue from East 7th Street to east of East 14th Street, including sidewalk, buffered bike lane,
storm sewer, and water main improvements.


FISCAL IMPACT:

Amount: $4,198,776.71 Construction Contract ($4,775,000 City Engineer's Estimate)

Funding Source: 2025-26 CIP, Page 162, LOSST Street Improvements, ST800, Being:
$3,763,576.96 Local Option Sales and Service Tax (LOSST) Funding and the remaining $435,199.75
from Des Moines Water Works

•   East Court Avenue from the Des Moines River to East 14th Street needs complete pavement
    reconstruction. There are also existing sidewalk curb ramps along East Court Avenue that need to
    be improved to meet Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requirements. Des Moines Water
    Works also needs to replace a water main under East Court Avenue.

•   Multiple mixed-use developments have recently been constructed along or near East Court Avenue.
    Additionally, traffic volumes have declined since the construction of Martin Luther King Jr.
    Parkway; therefore, the existing street cross section should be improved to meet the current and
    future needs of the corridor.

•   A virtual public meeting was held via Zoom on June 9, 2021, to present the initial proposed project
    concepts. Another virtual public meeting was held via Zoom on November 9, 2022, to present the
    final concepts and anticipated construction staging for Phase 1.

•   Due to the project size of the overall project, East Court Avenue is being constructed in two (2)
    separate phases:
    o Phase 1 – East Court Avenue from Des Moines River to east of East 7th Street (Finkbine
      Drive) – Completed Fall 2024
    o Phase 2 – East Court Avenue from east of East 7th Street (Finkbine Drive) to East 14th Street.

•   Phase 2 of this project will have a similar cross section to Phase 1, improving the East Court
    Avenue cross section to include one (1) travel lane in each direction with buffered bike lanes on
    both sides of the street.

•   Construction of Phase 2 is anticipated to begin in spring 2026 and be completed in spring 2027.
