AGENDA HEADING:

Hold public hearing on the intent to commence a public improvement project to construct the East
Army Post Road and Three Lakes Parkway Roundabout project and to authorize acquisition of the
necessary property interests, including agricultural land, for the project by gift, negotiation, or eminent
domain.


SYNOPSIS:

Recommend approval of authorization to proceed with acquisition of the necessary property interests
for the East Army Post Road and Three Lakes Parkway Roundabout project by gift, negotiation, or
eminent domain. This action is required by Iowa law when agricultural property is proposed for
acquisition, before the City can initiate the property acquisition process for this project under eminent
domain. Relocation assistance will not be required for this project.


FISCAL IMPACT: NO

Amount: To be determined after final design is completed.

Funding Source: 2026-27 CIP, Page 136, E. Army Post Road - SE 14th to SE 36th Corridor, ST293


ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

                                    In 2022, a traffic study was completed on a four (4)-mile section of the East Army Post Road
                                     corridor between US Highway 69 (US-69)/Southeast 14th Street and US Highway 65 (US-65).

                                    The study concluded that speed was the primary factor behind crashes resulting injuries
                                     occurring on the corridor.

   The study recommended traffic safety improvements on the corridor including installing
    roundabouts at multiple full access intersections, including Southeast 36th Street and Three
    Lakes Parkway.

   The roundabout at the intersection of East Army Post Road and Southeast 36th Street was
    constructed in 2025.

   This project includes the reconstruction of the intersection of East Army Post Road and Three
    Lakes Parkway into a roundabout. The roundabout is anticipated to be a single-lane roundabout
    with one (1) lane entering and exiting on each approach. Roundabout improvements will
    include curb and gutter and extended splitter islands to encourage reduced speeds as vehicles
    approach the roundabout.

   On September 2, 2026, under provisions of Iowa Code 6B.2A, notices of the proposed
    resolution authorizing acquisition of agricultural land by eminent domain for public
    improvement purposes were mailed to each affected agricultural land property owner and
    contract purchaser of record known to be occupying the properties required for the Project.

   The affected property owners have the right to attend the City Council Meeting on October 19,
    2026 to voice any objections to the proposed property interests to be acquired.

   Construction of this project is anticipated to start in spring 2027 and be completed by end of
    spring 2028.

   At this time, one (1) partial fee acquisition and one (1) temporary construction easement is
    anticipated to be needed from one (1) property owner to complete the construction of this
    project. Relocation assistance will not be required.
