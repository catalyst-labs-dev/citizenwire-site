AGENDA HEADING:

Accepting proposal and authorizing a contract with Crow’s Auto Service Inc. located at 826 21st Street
Des Moines, IA 50317 (Randy Crow, President) for vehicle towing and storage of impounded vehicles
and auctioneering services for the sale of abandoned vehicles.


SYNOPSIS:

The City Procurement Division released a Request for Proposal (RFP) on October 31, 2025 (W26-001)
for towing and storage services of impounded vehicles and auctioneering services of abandoned
vehicles. A selection committee reviewed each proposal received and recommended Crow’s Auto
Service, Inc. as having met the requirements stated in the RFP. The Procurement Division then began
negotiations with Crow’s Auto Services, Inc. to sign a contract for services. The resulting agreement
includes a term of three years (March 1, 2026, through February 28, 2029) with the total cost not to
exceed $300,000 as well as a single 1-year extension during which the vendor has agreed to a 5% cost
increase limit for the extended term.


FISCAL IMPACT:

Amount: $300,000

Funding Source: FY26 Adopted Operating Budget (printed) page 205 Property Management,
Contractual Services.


ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

The City of Des Moines first established a contract with Crow’s Auto Service, Inc. for the towing and
storage services on impounded vehicles in 2015 as the result of a Request for Proposal process. Each
three-year contract included the option of extension for additional years. The city has received
satisfactory service from the vendor under each contract and would like to accept another three-year
contract with a single one-year extension option.

Historically, the auction of abandoned vehicles was a service provided by Ronald Peck Auction Inc.,
However, Ronald Peck passed away and the company closed. The city reviewed options for another
company to provide auctioning services. It was determined that the most cost-effective option was to
contract Crow’s Auto Service, Inc. to provide the auctioneering services. Since the abandoned vehicles
were being stored at Crow’s Auto Service location on 826 21st Street in Des Moines, vehicles no
longer needed to be transported to another location for auction.
