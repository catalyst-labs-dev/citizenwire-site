Hearing on Birdland Park Phase 1A Improvements: Resolution approving plans, specifications, form
of contract documents, engineer’s estimate, receive and file bids, and designating lowest responsible
bidder as Iowa Earth Works, LLC, Joseph Jensen, Owner, $3,656,716.25.


SYNOPSIS:

This is a public hearing to allow for public input on the Birdland Park Phase 1A Improvements and
take the necessary actions as required by Iowa Code to authorize a contract for construction of the
proposed project. The Birdland Park Phase 1A Improvements project is the first of three (3) sub-phase
projects that make up Phase 1 of the community and stakeholder driven Birdland Park and Marina
Master Plan. This Phase 1A project includes elevating and realigning the Neal Smith Trail to reduce
the frequency of closing due to flooding, a trail spur connection to 2nd Avenue and the Central Place
Trail, two (2) open-air shelters with user amenities, three (3) dedicated fishing areas with two (2) being
American with Disabilities Act (ADA) accessible, ADA-compliant ingress and egress pathways,
power boat ramp and associated parking, ADA-inclusive pier and courtesy dock, storm sewer,
stormwater best management practices, natural resources improvements, and shoreline improvements.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

  •   On January 26, 2026, under Roll Call No. 26-0096, the City Council received and filed plans,
      specifications, form of contract documents, and City Engineer's estimate for the construction of
      the Birdland Park Phase 1A Improvements. Before the City Council can enter into a contract, it
      must hold a public hearing and approve the proposed plans, specifications, and form of contract

      Improvements, Activity ID 11-2023-003. The improvements include the construction of a
      Portland Cement Concrete (PCC) parking lot, boat ramp and dock, fishing piers, two (2) open-
      air shelters, storm sewer pipe, storm sewer manholes, storm sewer intakes, bioretention cells,
      PCC sidewalks and trail, PCC driveways, grading, cast-in-place concrete walls, electrical work,
      erosion control, surface restoration and other incidental items, all in accordance with the
      contract documents including Plan File No. 674-278/359, located at Birdland Park between 6th
      Avenue and 2nd Avenue, south of Birdland Drive.

         o Iowa Earth Works, LLC, Huxley, IA - Low Bid

      the City Council Chambers, 1st Floor, T.M. Franklin Cownie City Administration Building,

  •   A master plan for Birdland Park and Marina was developed in 2021 and was approved by the
      Parks and Recreation Board and adopted by the City Council. The master plan outlines a
      logical, highest value approach to implementing multiple phases of proposed improvements. In
      total the project was estimated to cost $54.4 million in 2021 and implementation is planned to
      spread across five (5) recommended phases.

  •   Informed by LiveDSM, the Parks and Recreation Department’s comprehensive plan, the
      Birdland master planning project has been community and stakeholder driven. To facilitate
      project feedback and achieve community support from the neighborhood and everyday park
      users, three (3) public meetings were held at different phases of the project’s development. The
      meetings garnered over 200 attendees and generated 350 plus follow-up survey responses.
      Additional public engagement for Phase 1 projects was ongoing throughout 2024 and 2025
      with all master plan stakeholders and in-person presentations to Union Park, River Bend,
      Highland Park/Oak Park, and Capitol Park Neighborhood Associations. The overarching
      sentiment was appreciation that the approved and adopted master plan is being implemented
      and realized.

•   Given the complexity and scale of the master plan, intentional sequencing or phasing is
    recommended to support improvements functioning on their own and are not contingent upon a
    subsequent phase.

•   Birdland Park and Marina Master Plan - Phase 1A Project Improvements Summary:
       o Realign, elevate, and reconstruct the flood-prone segment of the Neal Smith Trail
         (approximately 0.4 miles)
               ▪   Over 160,000 trail uses were recorded in 2024 by a nearby infrared counter
       o Develop a trail spur connection to 2nd Avenue Bridge and Central Place Trail
         (approximately 600 linear feet)
       o Develop two (2) open-air shelters with electrical service, bike racks, grill pits, and
         seating
       o Develop three (3) fishing locations (two (2) ADA-accessible, one (1) hike-in)
       o Develop a new powerboat ramp and associated parking area with:
               ▪   Ideal ingress/egress launching and docking approach angle
               ▪   ADA-accessible floating courtesy dock
               ▪   Parking with tree plantings, stormwater BMPs, and LED lighting
               ▪   23 standard stalls, two (2) ADA stalls, 20 standard trailer stalls, and one (1)
                   ADA trailer stall
       o Planting over 100 native and diverse trees and shrubs, and approximately 3.5 acres of
         prairie and savanna vegetation to broadly support water quality and wildlife habitat
       o Install approximately 1,800 feet of shoreline improvements to limit erosion and enhance
         flood resilience

•   A dedicated project webpage offers a repository of project information including links to digital
    public meetings, video recordings from prior meetings, downloadable exhibits such as the
    presentations slides from public meetings and the master plan concept, and updates on the plan
    adoption process. The project webpage can be found on the City’s website under Parks and
    Recreation Department by navigating to current planning projects.

•   In late 2022, a professional service agreement was completed with SmithGroup, Inc. to provide
    engineering design for two sites within Birdland Park, Phase 1A and Phase 1B. Phase 1B is
    approximately 95% complete with design and is expected to bid early spring 2026 pending City
    Council approval.

•   This Phase 1A project is anticipated to begin in spring 2026 and be completed in late spring
    2027.
