AGENDA HEADING:

Resolution approving the First Amendment to the Grant Agreement with Iowa Soccer Development
Foundation, Inc. (ISDF).


SYNOPSIS:

This amendment to the existing Grant Agreement provides for a $656,074/year grant to ISDF toward
its efforts in leasing property on which they plan to build Iowa’s first professional soccer stadium and a
multi-use public plaza on a portion of the City’s 38-acre Superfund site, Dico. ISDF will be eligible
for the grant upon full compliance with all lease terms. That lease was approved on June 8, 2026, and
outlined an annual lease payment of $656,075 for 25 years.

The amendment also addresses the timing of the previously approved $7 million grant. The
amendment provides a schedule for payments: $3 million upon 75% completion of the stadium and
plaza or July 1, 2028; $2 million upon 90% completion of the stadium and plaza; and $2 million upon
100% completion of the stadium and plaza, to be paid within 30 days following issuance of the
Certificate of Occupancy. The Lease Agreement with ISDF includes a requirement for a completion
bond or other comparable security prior to commencement of construction and maintained through
substantial completion of the improvements and issuance of a permanent certificate of occupancy.


FISCAL IMPACT:

Amount: $656,074/year in a grant, which will offset the $656,075/year in annual lease revenues paid
by ISDF to the City.

Funding Source: The lease revenues will be paid by ISDF to the City; the grants from the City to ISDF
will be paid for with the lease revenue proceeds received by the City from ISDF.


ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

                                  •   ISDF intends to lease a portion of the City-owned Dico site, a Superfund site located south of
                                      Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) Parkway between Southwest 16th Street and the Raccoon River,
                                      in order to build a stadium designed for soccer and a public plaza. The grant facilitates
                                      productive reuse of land owned by the City that otherwise may remain underutilized.

                                  •   The stadium will include a seating capacity of no fewer than 5,500 seats, and will be fully
                                      constructed along with the public plaza by December 31, 2029.
