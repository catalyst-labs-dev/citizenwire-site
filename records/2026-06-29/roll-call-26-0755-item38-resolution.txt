               APPROVING FIRST AMENDMENT TO GRANT AGREEMENT WITH
                  THE IOWA SOCCER DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION, INC.

             WHEREAS, the City of Des Moines, Iowa ("City") and the Iowa Soccer Development
     Foundation, Inc., ("ISDF") (collectively, the "Parties") entered into a Grant Agreement
     ("Agreement"), as authorized by the Des Moines City Council on December 8, 2025, by Roll Call
     No. 25-1605, in which the City agreed to provide a financial grant in the total amount of Seven
     Million Dollars ($7,000,000.00) to assist with the construction of a soccer stadium and multiuse
     public plaza (the "Project") located on approximately 24.73 acres of the Des Moines TCE
     Superfund Site pursuant to a Ground Lease Agreement (the "Lease"); and


            WHEREAS, the Parties now wish to amend the Agreement to modify the timing for
     disbursement of the funds (the "First Amendment"); and


             WHEREAS, the First Amendment requires the City to disburse the Grant funds in
     accordance with the following schedule, provided that ISDF is in full compliance with all Lease
     terms related to the constmction of the Project:


                 First Disbursement: $3,000,000.00 upon the later of (i) 75% completion of the Project
                 or (ii) July 1,2028;

                 Second Disbursement: $2,000,000.00 upon the later of (i) 90% completion of the
                 Project, or (ii) July 1, 2029;

                 Final Disbursement: $2,000,000.00 upon 100% completion of the Project, to be paid
                 within thirty (30) days following issuance of the Certificate of Completion.

             WHEREAS, the Parties further wish to amend the Agreement to provide that, upon
     issuance of the Certificate of Completion, the City shall make annual payments to ISDF in the
     amount of Six Hundred Fifty-Six Thousand and Seventy-Four Dollars ($656,074.00) for a period
     of twenty-five (25) years; such payment being personal to ISDF and conditioned upon the Stadium
     continuing to be used for soccer throughout the payment term.


             NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of the City of Des
     Moines, Iowa, as follows:


              1. The First Amendment to the Grant Agreement by and between the City and ISDF in
                 the form on file in the office of the City Clerk is hereby approved;

              2. The Mayor is authorized to execute the Amendment on behalf of the City and the City
                 Clerk is authorized to attest to the Mayor's signature on said document;
