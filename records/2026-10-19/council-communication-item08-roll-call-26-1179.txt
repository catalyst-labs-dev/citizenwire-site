AGENDA HEADING:

Approving Change Order No. 2 with Grimes Asphalt and Paving Corporation for additional work on
the 2026 Hot-Mix Asphalt (HMA) Resurfacing Program Contract 1, in the amount of $478,429.70.


SYNOPSIS:

Recommend approval of Change Order No. 2 in the amount of $478,429.70 with Grimes Asphalt and
Paving Corporation, Jeffry Rasmussen, President, 5550 Northeast 22nd Street, Des Moines, Iowa,
50313, for additional work on the 2026 HMA Resurfacing Program Contract 1 which includes
resurfacing Hickman Road from Martin Luther King, Jr. Parkway to 16th Street near Broadlawns
Hospital, including re-configuration of the lanes from a four (4)-lane cross section to three (3)-lane
cross section, which will accommodate proposed parking lot, site, and pedestrian improvements to be
implemented by Broadlawns Hospital.


FISCAL IMPACT:

Amount: $478,429.70

Funding Source: 2026-27 CIP, Page 109, Public Works – Asphalt Road Improvements, ST003


ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

                                    This project provides for the resurfacing of approximately 3.6 centerline miles of roadways
                                     citywide, utilizing pavement surface scarification and HMA overlay to improve the surface
                                     condition and to extend the useful service life of the streets.

                                    On March 23, 2026, under Roll Call No. 26-0392, the City Council held a hearing on the
                                     2026 HMA Resurfacing Program Contract 1, designating the lowest responsive, responsible

   On June 24, 2026, the City and Grimes Asphalt and Paving Corporation executed Change
    Order No. 1 for an amount of $1,760, to add a quadrupole loop at East 9th Street and Hull
    Avenue.

   In 2023, the traffic study of the Hickman Road corridor from Martin Luther King Jr.
    Parkway to Franklin Avenue was completed.

   Hickman Road between Martin Luther King Jr. Parkway and 6th Avenue is primarily a four
    (4)-lane undivided road with about 10,000 vehicles per day.

   The traffic study concluded that a three (3)-lane street cross section could operate at a
    similar level to the existing four (4)-lane corridor with minimal added delay despite the
    reduction from the four (4)-lane cross-section to three (3)-lane.

   Change Order No. 2 adds items to resurface Hickman Road from Martin Luther King, Jr.
    Parkway to 16th Street near Broadlawns Hospital, including re-configuration of the lanes
    from a four (4)-lane cross section to three (3)-lane cross section, which will accommodate
    proposed parking lot, site, and pedestrian improvements to be implemented by Broadlawns
    Hospital.
