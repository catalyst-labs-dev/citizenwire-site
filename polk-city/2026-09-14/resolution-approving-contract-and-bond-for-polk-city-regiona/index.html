      What this means
      Bonds and notes
      Borrowing. The city sells bonds or notes to raise money now for projects and repays it with interest over years. General obligation (GO) debt is repaid from property taxes.
      Consent agenda
      A list of routine items the council approves together in one vote, without separate discussion. Any member can ask to pull an item out for its own vote.
