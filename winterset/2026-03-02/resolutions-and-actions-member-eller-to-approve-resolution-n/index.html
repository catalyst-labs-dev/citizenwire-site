At which time…”. Carried unanimously (roll call); names not recorded. Amount in the record: $16.

      What this means (Tax increment financing (TIF)): Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.
