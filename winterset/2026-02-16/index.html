        What this means

        Resolution: A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 5Andrew J. Barden, City Administrator/City Clerk: Member Eller to approve Change Order #3 for WWTF seconded by Council Member Fletcher. There being no further discussion;Carried unanimously (roll call) · Count only · Minutes, p. 4
        What this means

        Change order: An approved change to a construction contract the city already signed. It can raise or lower the price, or change the work or the schedule.

      • Vote 6Member Cook to approve Pay App #7 for WWTF seconded by Council Member Orr. There being no further discussion;Carried unanimously (roll call) · Count only · Minutes, p. 5
        What this means

        Pay application: A contractor’s request to be paid for work finished so far on an approved project. Cities often hold back a share, called retainage, until the job is complete.
