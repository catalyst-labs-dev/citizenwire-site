      • Vote 4Public Hearing: Member Orr to enter into public hearing; the motion was seconded by Council Member Cook. There being no further discussion;Carried unanimously (roll call) · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Public hearing. A set time at a meeting when anyone can speak to the council about a proposal before it decides. Hearings are required by law for some actions, such as budgets, rezoning and borrowing.

      • Vote 5Resolutions and Actions: Member Fletcher to approve Resolution No. 2026-34 a Resolution to Increase Garbage Rates for Residential Customers of Waste Management (WM) seconded by Council Member Fairholm. There being no further discussion;Carried unanimously (roll call) · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 6a private individual, Deputy City Clerk: Member Cook to approve Pay App #12 for WWTF seconded by Council Member Fairholm. There being no further discussion;Carried unanimously (roll call) · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Pay application. A contractor’s request to be paid for work finished so far on an approved project. Cities often hold back a share, called retainage, until the job is complete.

      • Vote 7Member Orr to table the matter regarding Ordinance No #674 an Ordinance Amending Section 4.12003 of the Winterset Municipal Code to Amend the Definition of the “In Home Day Care Provider” for a later date, which has not been set at this time. The motion was seconded by Council Member Fairholm. There being no further discussion;Carried unanimously (roll call) · Count only · Minutes, p. 5

        What this means: Tabling. Setting an item aside for now. A tabled item can be brought back at a later meeting. It is not a final yes or no.
