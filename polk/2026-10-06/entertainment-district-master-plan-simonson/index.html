      Polk County › Oct 6, 2026 meeting › Item 29

      The supervisors approved an agreement with Simonson & Associates for master planning and market analysis of a possible entertainment district near the Iowa Events Center, by a 3–0 vote, with one supervisor passing.

      Polk County Board of Supervisors · Oct 6, 2026 · Item 29

      Approved the agreement with Simonson & Associates LLC for environmental, master planning, rendering/animation and market analysis work. Supervisor Connolly passed; Supervisor Hockensmith was absent. Adopted.

      More from the record

      The county intends to do master planning and market analysis for "a potential new entertainment district north and south of the convention complex." The memo says the district would turn underutilized land into a mixed-use destination and expand jobs and the tax base (the county’s stated expectations). The fiscal note lists $175,000 to $200,000, with $25,000 from the City of Des Moines and $50,000 from Knapp Properties. Two speakers commented on the item; one also submitted written questions, which were received and filed. The County Administrator discussed the agreement’s background and the county’s procurement process.
