      The supervisors approved a $400,000 forgivable loan to FOCUSS to start a 24/7 youth crisis observation center, contingent on state matching funds, by a 4–0 vote.

      More from the record

      FOCUSS asked for $400,000 as part of a $1 million startup package. The county’s money serves as the local match needed to unlock funding from Iowa Health and Human Services (HHS), and is contingent on HHS matching funds. The center would offer a 24/7 point of access for youth in crisis, with assessment, crisis intervention and care coordination for up to 23 hours. Two FOCUSS representatives spoke during public comment, and four letters in support were received and filed.
