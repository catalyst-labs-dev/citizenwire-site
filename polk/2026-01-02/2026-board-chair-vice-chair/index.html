      Polk County Board of Supervisors · Jan 2, 2026 · Item 2

      Adopted resolutions electing the 2026 chair and vice chair. Names elected: Not in the record (minutes). The vice chair resolution passed 4–1, with Hockensmith voting no.

      More from the record

      Each January the board elects its chair and vice chair. Who was elected is not stated in the minutes. Board documents later in the year list Matt McCoy as Chair. For the chair vote, the minutes list all five supervisors as voting yea and also list Hockensmith and Connolly as voting no, so the exact count is unclear in the record.
