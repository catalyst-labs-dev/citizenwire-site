      More from the record

      The board held budget deliberations in January and February, a public budget forum on Feb 17, a public hearing on proposed property taxes on Mar 24 (three speakers commented), and a public hearing on the budget on Apr 21 (one speaker commented). The budget carries out wage and benefit terms from collective bargaining agreements and Board resolutions. The fiscal note lists $393,781,389 in anticipated expense, $393,055,901 in anticipated revenue, and 3.83 new positions.
