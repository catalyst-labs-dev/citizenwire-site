      The supervisors approved the first reading of a rezoning near NE 14th Street in Saylor Township from general commercial to light industrial, by a 3–2 vote, after the county zoning commission recommended denial.

      More from the record

      About 5.75 acres of vacant land west of an existing business park on NE 14th Street (Hwy 69), north of NE 66th Avenue, next to a single-family neighborhood to the west. The owner, Woodside Business Park LLC, asked to rezone it from "GC" General Commercial to "LI" Light Industrial to build an industrial business park for contractor tenant suites. County staff wrote that the request "is not in accordance with the Comprehensive Plan" and that industrial uses would be "incompatible with surrounding land uses and neighborhood character." The Polk County Zoning Commission voted 5–0 (2 absent) on Nov 24, 2025 to recommend denial.

      The alternative

      Deny the rezoning, as the zoning commission recommended. The land would stay General Commercial.
