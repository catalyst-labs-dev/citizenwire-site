      1 · What was decided

      The supervisors approved the first reading of a rezoning near NE 14th Street in Saylor Township from general commercial to light industrial, by a 3–2 vote, after the county zoning commission recommended denial.

      • Polk County
      • Jan 13, 2026
      • Zoning · Land use
      What this is about

      About 5.75 acres of vacant land west of an existing business park on NE 14th Street (Hwy 69), north of NE 66th Avenue, next to a single-family neighborhood to the west. The owner, Woodside Business Park LLC, asked to rezone it from "GC" General Commercial to "LI" Light Industrial to build an industrial business park for contractor tenant suites. County staff wrote that the request "is not in accordance with the Comprehensive Plan" and that industrial uses would be "incompatible with surrounding land uses and neighborhood character." The Polk County Zoning Commission voted 5–0 (2 absent) on Nov 24, 2025 to recommend denial. Agenda packet

      The alternative

      Deny the rezoning, as the zoning commission recommended. The land would stay General Commercial.

      The decision

      Approved the ordinance on first reading. The minutes then record the second reading on Jan 27, 2026 and the third reading on Feb 10, 2026, each passing 5–0. Minutes (saved copy)

      What this means
      Ordinance readings
      An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.
      Rezoning
      Zoning sets what can be built or done on each piece of land, such as homes, shops or industry. Changing it is done by ordinance, usually after a planning commission review and a public hearing.
