      More from the record

      The notes pay for capital improvements and housing programs approved in the FY 25/26 budget, its first-quarter amendment and FY 26/27 budget deliberations. Public hearings on the borrowing were held Apr 7, 2026. Total project costs are estimated at $13,544,604; the memo says the borrowing "will be sized to actual project costs and will not exceed $15.0M," will be fully paid off in June 2027, and carries an estimated 3.5% interest rate. The list of projects is in the agenda packet.
