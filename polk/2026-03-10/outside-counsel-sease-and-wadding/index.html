      Polk County › Mar 10, 2026 meeting › Item 15

      The supervisors approved hiring the law firm Sease and Wadding to represent the county in a legal matter, by a 3–2 vote.

      Polk County Board of Supervisors · Mar 10, 2026 · Item 15

      Approved the Letter of Engagement with Sease and Wadding. The firm will work under a contingency agreement. Passed 3–2; voting no: Tom Hockensmith, Angela Connolly.

      More from the record

      The county wants to negotiate over a matter that "may result in litigation if negotiations prove unfruitful." The memo says the County Attorney’s Office cannot represent the county in it "due to professional considerations." What the matter is: Not in the record.
