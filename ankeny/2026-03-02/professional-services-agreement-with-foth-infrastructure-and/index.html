      What this is about

      Item 26 of the consent agenda, recorded in the minutes as: “Professional Services Agreement with Foth Infrastructure and Environment, LLC for the 2026 construction engineering services on the West First Street Widening and Improvements - Phase 2 project in an amount not to exceed $502,520.00”. The council approved the whole consent agenda in one vote. Why this has a full page: $100,000 or more.
