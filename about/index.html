      About Citizen Wire

      Citizen Wire is an independent, nonpartisan project based in Des Moines.

      Citizen Wire lists what local officials decided and how each member voted, sourced to the public record. Where the record does not show something, the page says "Not in the record." Citizen Wire does not endorse or rate candidates or ballot questions. Corrections are added as dated notes, and the original stays visible.
