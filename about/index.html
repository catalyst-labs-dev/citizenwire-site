About Citizen Wire

Citizen Wire is a nonpartisan news source about local government. We explain decisions in plain language.

Sources

Every fact links to an official source, like meeting video (with a time stamp), agendas, minutes, or public documents. If something is not in the official record, we say so. We do not guess.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it fast and say what changed and when. To report an error, message @Citizen_Wire on X.

No party skew

We focus on decisions and how they affect people, not on parties. We use the same words for every side and let each side explain its reasons in its own words.

Privacy

This site has no ads, no trackers, and no cookies.
