      General election

      Polls open 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. What’s on the ballot is posted by the Polk County Auditor closer to the date.

      Local contests in Polk County, listed in the order the County Auditor’s candidate list uses. The order on your own ballot may differ. Ballots vary by precinct; check your own sample ballot. Candidates are listed equally. Citizen Wire does not endorse or rate candidates.

      Vote: where, when, how

      • Find your polling place: Iowa Secretary of State lookup
      • Sample ballots by precinct and early voting sites: Polk County Auditor (saved copy)
      • Register or update: Polk County Auditor. The pre-registration deadline was Oct 19; you can still register when you vote early in person or on Election Day.
      • Early voting at the election office began Oct 14, during regular hours, plus Sat, Oct 24 (8 AM to 4 PM) and Sat, Oct 31 (8 AM to 5 PM). Details. Satellite sites: list (saved copy)
      • Absentee ballots must be received by Nov 3 at 8:00 PM. Track your absentee ballot

      City of Urbandale · Where: Urbandale precincts

      “Shall the City of Urbandale, State of Iowa, issue its General Obligation Bonds or Capital Loan Notes in an amount not exceeding the amount of $25,000,000 for the purpose of paying costs of designing, constructing, equipping, and furnishing a new police station, with related site improvements?”

      Public Measure S

      Saydel Community School District · Where: Saydel CSD areas (incl. Des Moines 1, 3, 5; Saylor; Delaware; Ankeny 21)

      “Shall the Board of Directors of the Saydel Community School District in the County of Polk, State of Iowa, be authorized to contract indebtedness and issue General Obligation Bonds in an amount not to exceed $26,900,000 to provide funds to build, furnish, and equip an addition to Cornell Elementary for kindergarten and early childhood education, including a playground and site improvements; to remodel, repair, furnish, and equip Cornell Elementary, Woodside Middle School, and the High School, including HVAC, electrical, and building improvements; for improvements to the High School emergency generator system and sewer line; and to remodel, repair, furnish, and equip athletic facilities, including improvements to the softball concession and restrooms facility, football field turf replacement, and softball and baseball field turf installation?”

      Yes / No · Sample ballot (saved copy)

      Public Measure BQ

      Madrid Community School District · Where: Madrid CSD areas in Polk (Madison 1, Sheldahl 1)

      “Shall the Board of Directors of the Madrid Community School District in the Counties of Boone, Dallas, and Polk, State of Iowa, be authorized to contract indebtedness and issue General Obligation Bonds in an amount not to exceed $4,300,000 to provide funds to build, furnish and equip additions to its existing Junior-Senior High School, including a Career Academy addition, with related remodeling and improvements and site improvements?”
