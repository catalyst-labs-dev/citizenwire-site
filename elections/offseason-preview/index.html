      Get ready to vote

      Your checklist

      • Register or update
        Polk County Auditor. If you miss the pre-registration deadline, Election Day registration is available.
      • Check your registration
        Iowa Secretary of State lookup
      • Find your polling place
        Iowa Secretary of State lookup. Polling places can change, so check before each election. For special elections, confirm with the County Auditor.
      • Vote absentee or early
        Polk County Auditor · Track your absentee ballot
