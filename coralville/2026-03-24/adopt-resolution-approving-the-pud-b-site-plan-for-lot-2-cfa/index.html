      Adopt Resolution approving the PUD-B Site Plan for Lot 2, CFA Addition, Coralville Iowa

      Coralville City Council · Mar 24, 2026 · Zoning

      Recorded in the minutes as “Moved by Knudson, seconded by Peterson to adopt Resolution approving the PUD-B Site Plan for Lot 2, CFA Addition, Coralville Iowa, was introduced by Freeman, seconded by Huynh. A roll call vote was taken. Councilperson…”. Passed 3–0; Yes: Knudson, Huynh, Peterson.

      What this means (Planned unit development (PUD)): A custom zoning district with rules written for one project, instead of the standard district rules.
