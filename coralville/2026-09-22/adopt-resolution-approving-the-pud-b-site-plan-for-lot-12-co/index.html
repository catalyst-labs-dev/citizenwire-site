      Adopt Resolution approving the PUD-B Site Plan for Lot 12, Coral Ridge Commerce Park Part 2,…

      Coralville City Council · Sep 22, 2026 · Zoning · Parks

      Recorded in the minutes as “Moved by Huynh, seconded by Peterson to adopt Resolution approving the PUD-B Site Plan for Lot 12, Coral Ridge Commerce Park Part 2, Phase Ten. Coralville, Iowa. A roll call vote was taken”. Passed 4–0; Yes: Peterson, Freeman, Knudson, Huynh. Abstained: Vogelzang.

      What this means (Planned unit development (PUD)): A custom zoning district with rules written for one project, instead of the standard district rules.
