      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Mayor Laurie Goodrich proclaimed February 2026 as “American Heart Month.” a private individual with the Rotary Kerber Heart Safe Community Campaign reported their campaign has been Johnson County wide since 2019 and they have enjoyed tremendous support from Coralville since the very beginning. Motion: Moved by Knudson, seconded by Huynh to adopt Resolution terminating the Agreement with Flock Group and rescinding Police Policy regarding Automated License Plate Readers. A roll call vote was taken”. The minutes add: “The campaign is partnered with Johnson County Ambulance Service, local governments and Johnson County. Merchant noted they have made some progress by increasing the Johnson County survival rate with good neurological function from 14.9% last year to 19.8% which is terrific, but we can do better. They want to put an emphasis on providing multi-unit housing with AEDs because 70% of sudden cardiac arrest …”

      Motion as recorded: “Moved by Knudson, seconded by Huynh to adopt Resolution terminating the Agreement with Flock Group and rescinding Police Policy regarding Automated License Plate Readers. A roll call vote was taken”.
