      Coralville City Council · Jun 9, 2026 · Taxes and fees · Budget

      Recorded in the minutes as “RECREATION CENTER PROJECT FINANCING: Moved by Huynh, seconded by Freeman to adopt Resolution authorizing and approving a Loan Agreement, awarding the sale, providing for the issuance of Taxable General Obligation Urban Renewal Bonds, Series 2026B.…”. Passed 3–0; names not recorded.

      What this means (Tax increment financing (TIF)): Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.
