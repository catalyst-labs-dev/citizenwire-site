      Recorded in the minutes as “3. In any event, no vehicles shall be stored or parked outside for more than a total of 30 days. Motion: Moved by Knudsonm, seconded by Peterson to adopt Ordinance 2020-1009, as previously amended, rezoning certain…”. Passed 5–0; names not recorded.

      What this means (Rezoning): Zoning sets what can be built or done on each piece of land, such as homes, shops or industry.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “3. In any event, no vehicles shall be stored or parked outside for more than a total of 30 days. Motion: Moved by Knudsonm, seconded by Peterson to adopt Ordinance 2020-1009, as previously amended, rezoning certain property located within the corporate limits of the City of Coralville, Johnson County, Iowa and generally known as Auditor’s Parcel 2025104 from C-PUD 2, Commercial Planned Unit Development Two District, to C-2, Arterial Commercial District for 2nd consideration. A roll call vote was taken”. The minutes add: “The Board of Adjustment considers conditional use permits, and it is their role to review site plans and building design for automobile repair businesses in the C-2 Arterial Commercial District. The Board of Adjustment approved the Conditional Use Permit for Caliber Collision with the following conditions. The first three are the provisions that are listed under item f, item 4 is no outside storage material or equipment at any time. The Conditional Use Permit is contingent upon city staff final approval of the site plan and building design and the site shall always be maintained in …”

      Motion as recorded: “Moved by Knudsonm, seconded by Peterson to adopt Ordinance 2020-1009, as previously amended, rezoning certain property located within the corporate limits of the City of Coralville, Johnson County, Iowa and generally known as Auditor’s Parcel 2025104 from C-PUD 2, Commercial Planned Unit Development Two District, to C-2, Arterial Commercial District for 2nd consideration. A roll call vote was taken”.

      The minutes give only the count (5–0 (count from the minutes’ statement “Votes are … Ayes … unless otherwise noted”)), so individual votes are Not in the record. Vote line as printed: “Motion carried 5/0.”.
