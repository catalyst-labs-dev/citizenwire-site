      • Vote 31. Resolution Approving the Award for the 1,500 Foot Parallel Taxiway Extension. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Mahler approving the award for the bid, upon FAA concurrence and available funding for the 1,500 Foot Parallel Taxiway Extension project to Peterson Contractors, Inc. for a total amount of $1,209,990.75. Bids were received for the 1,500 Foot Parallel Taxiway Extension project at 2:00 p.m. on May 14th. Five bids were received, and all bidders were responsive. The Engineer’s Estimate for the base bid of the project was $1,600,000. Bids are summarized below: The bid received from Peterson Contractors, Inc. of Reinbeck for the base bid was 24% below the engineer’s estimate for the project, and was determined to be the lowest responsive, responsible bidder. Bolton & Menk, Inc. reviewed the bid documents submitted and checked references on this bidder and was satisfied with the responses given. Bolton & Menk recommended the award of the 1,500 Foot Parallel Taxiway Extension Project to Peterson Contractors, Inc. for a total amount of $1,209,990.75. This resolution awards the bid, upon FAA concurrence and available fundingCarried unanimously · Count only · $1,600,000 · Minutes · Full page →

      • Vote 51. Resolution Approving a Temporary Construction Easement and Permanent Drainage Easement. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Klein approving the Temporary Construction Easement and Permanent Drainage Easement with Marco Properties, LLC. Staff deemed it necessary to have a temporary construction easement for the Soumas Avenue project on property owned by Marco Properties, LLC. Such temporary construction easement will grant access to this area during construction. It was also deemed necessary to have a permanent drainage easement for drainage infrastructure being installed with the Soumas Avenue project on property owned by Marco Properties, LLC.         What this means: Easement. A right for the city or a utility to use part of someone’s land for a set purpose, such as pipes or access. The owner keeps the land.

      • Vote 111. Revenues from an Electric System Franchise Fee. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Klein approving and adopting a Revenue Purpose Statement for Expenditure of Fee Revenues from an Electric System Franchise Fee. This resolution approves the revenue purpose statement pursuance to the provisions of Section 364.2 subsection (4)(f) of the Code of Iowa. The Revenue Statement needed to be approved to outline the purposes for which the City shall use the electric system franchise fee revenues that will be received if the proposed ordinance is adopted. This proposed ordinance adopts a new chapter to include Guthrie County Rural Electric Cooperative Association with a franchise agreement for 25 years and set the franchise fee charge on the gross receipts of the sale of electricity to customers within the city limits of Perry, to a total of 4%. This resolution also authorizes the publication of such Statement as requiredCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes · Full page →
