      • Vote 51. Resolution Approving a Second Amendment to the RISE Grant Application. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Klein approving the second amendment to the RISE Grant Application. After additional discussions with Iowa DOT, and further alternatives analysis completed for the Willis Avenue/Highway 141 intersection, and in order to further the safety goals of Iowa DOT’s systems, it was concluded and agreed upon by Iowa DOT that a roundabout would be the best solution for the intersection. Such conclusion created an increase in the cost for the project, bringing the Willis Avenue/Highway 141 Intersection Improvements project to $2,626,163 (previously $1,930,646) or 60% of total eligible costs, with the local match increasing to $1,750,775 (previously $1,287,098) or 40% of the total eligible costs for the project. This resolution approves the amendment to the application for the increased project costs and authorizes the additional local match, at an increase of $450,700Carried unanimously · Count only · $2,626,163 · Minutes · Full page →

      • Vote 8Moved by Mahler, seconded by Wolling authorizing the publication of the intent to procure for technical services for the administration of a Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Housing-Roof Replacement Program, if funded by the Iowa Economic Development Authority (IEDA). The City of Perry intends to apply to the Iowa Economic Development Authority (IEDA) for a Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Housing-Roof Replacement Program, which provides financial assistance for the replacement of up to six (6) roof, fascia, soffit and gutter systems. The homes must be owned and occupied by low- moderate income individuals. The grant requires a local match, and each project may receive up to $24,000 in rehabilitation funding. This resolution authorizes the City has established and will publish a Request For Proposal to seek and secure Technical Services for the implementation and administration of a Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Housing-Roof Replacement Program, if funded by the Iowa Economic Development Authority (IEDA)Carried unanimously · Count only · $24,000 · Minutes
