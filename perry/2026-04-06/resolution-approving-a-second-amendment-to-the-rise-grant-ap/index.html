      Resolution Approving a Second Amendment to the RISE Grant Application

      Perry City Council · Apr 6, 2026 · Getting around

      Recorded in the minutes as “1. Resolution Approving a Second Amendment to the RISE Grant Application. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Klein approving the second amendment to the RISE Grant Application. After additional discussions with Iowa…”. Carried unanimously; names not recorded. Amount in the record: $2,626,163.

      What this means (Resolution): A formal council decision, usually made in one vote.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Resolution Approving a Second Amendment to the RISE Grant Application. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Klein approving the second amendment to the RISE Grant Application. After additional discussions with Iowa DOT, and further alternatives analysis completed for the Willis Avenue/Highway 141 intersection, and in order to further the safety goals of Iowa DOT’s systems, it was concluded and agreed upon by Iowa DOT that a roundabout would be the best solution for the intersection. Such conclusion created an increase in the cost for the project, bringing the Willis Avenue/Highway 141 Intersection Improvements project to $2,626,163 (previously $1,930,646) or 60% of total eligible costs, with the local match increasing to $1,750,775 (previously $1,287,098) or 40% of the total eligible costs for the project. This resolution approves the amendment to the application for the increased project costs and authorizes the additional local match, at an increase of $450,700”.
