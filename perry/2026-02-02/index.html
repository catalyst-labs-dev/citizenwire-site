      • Vote 31. Approval of Revised GAX #16 for CDBG Contract Number 23-HSGU-004. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Mahler approving revised GAX #16. GAX #16 was originally approved by the Council during their December 15, 2025, regular city council meeting in the amount of $14,112.00, but due to new regulations from the Iowa Economic Development Authority (IEDA) it has extended the time period in which signed GAX’s are approved. GAX #16 had not been authorized yet by IEDA due to GAX #15 not having been finalized, therefore a revision was needed on GAX #16 to increase the draw to include an additional invoice. Revised GAX #16 in the amount of $34,732.00 is a drawdown on the CDBG Upper Story Housing Conversion Grant Contract Number 23-HSGU-004. The City of Perry accepted the CDBG Upper Story Housing Conversion Grant per Resolution 041723B for a conversion taking place at the property located at 1306 2nd Street, owned by the Nudgers Kerns Managers, LLCCarried unanimously · Count only · $34,732 · Minutes
        What this means

        Resolution: A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 41. Grant Contract 23-HSGU-004. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Klein approving Amendment Number One to the CDBG Upper Story Housing Conversion Grant Contract 23-HSGU-004. The City of Perry was awarded a Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) for an Upper Story Housing Conversion Grant by the Iowa Economic Development Authority (IEDA) on February 15, 2023, and per Resolution 041723B accepted such grant. Since accepting the Grant, it has been determined that an amendment was needed to extend the Period of Performance (PoP) end date on the Agreement from February 28, 2026, to May 31, 2026. This resolution approves the supplied Amendment Number One document for Contract 23-HSGU-004 dated January 26, 2026, along with the accompanied letter from the Mayor detailing the request dated January 22, 2026Carried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

      • Vote 5Moved by Klein, seconded by Mahler to take additional action on the General Obligation Loan Agreements, Combine Loaning Agreements, set a date for sale of General Obligation Corporate Purpose Bonds, Series 2026 and authorize the use of the Preliminary Official Statement. Pursuant to the provisions of Section 384.24A of the Code of Iowa, the City of Perry proposed to enter into an Essential Purpose Loan Agreement in an amount to not exceed $6,775,000 for the purpose of paying the costs, to that extent, of (a) constructing street, water system, sanitary sewer system, storm water drainage and sidewalk improvements; (b) acquiring and installing street lighting, signage and signalization improvements; and (c) undertaking runway improvements at the municipal airport and a General Obligation Corporate Purpose Loan Agreement in an amount to not exceed $405,000 for the purpose of paying the costs, to that extent, of constructing and equipping a public works/snow removal equipment facility, and pursuant to law and duly published notice of the proposed action has held a hearing thereon on February 2, 2026. A Preliminary Official Statement (P.O.S.) was prepared to facilitate the sale of the Bonds, to be issued in evidence of the obligation of the City under the Loan Agreement, and where it was necessary to make provision for the approval of the P.O.S. and to authorize its use by PFM Financial Advisors LLC, as municipal advisor to the City. This Resolution combines the Loan Agreements, authorizes the P.O.S as prepared by PFM Financial Advisors, LLC, and sets the date of sale for the bondsCarried unanimously · Count only · $6,775,000 · Minutes · Full page →

      • Vote 61. Approval of a Proposal with DGR Engineering for Professional Services. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Wolling approving Professional Services for an Independent Fee Estimate with DGR Engineering. In order to receive Federal Funding through the FAA they require that an Independent Fee Estimate (IFE) be performed in accordance with the FAA Advisory Circular 150/5100-14 Architectural, Engineering, and Planning Consultant Services. These Professional Services are for an Independent Fee Estimate (IFE) to be performed for the 1,500' Parallel Taxiway Extension - Design and Construction Engineering fees for the project by DGR Engineering in accordance with the FAA for a lump sum amount of $4,600, reimbursable under the grant with the FAA as project expenseCarried unanimously · Count only · $4,600 · Minutes · Full page →
      • Vote 71. Approval of a Proposed Amendment #1 to the Services Agreement with IMEG. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Wolling approving Amendment #1 to the Services Agreement with IMEG to include the Perry Elementary School project. Per Resolution 100625F the City entered into a Services Agreement with IMEG to provide plan review services for the new JBS and A.D.R Axles project. Since entering into such Agreement, it was determined that IMEG services are also needed for the Perry Elementary School project for review services of the electrical and fire protection portion of the plan review. This Amendment adds the Perry Elementary School project to the Services Agreement at a cost not to exceed $6,700. Staff are also working with IMEG on reviewing a possible Master Services Agreement in order to engage their plan review services on an on-call basis for possible future review by CouncilCarried unanimously · Count only · $6,700 · Minutes
