      • Vote 51. Resolution Supporting the Home Base Iowa Initiative. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Mahler supporting the Home Base Iowa Initiative. Home Base Iowa (HBI) is a statewide program designed to connect military veterans and transitioning service members with career opportunities in Iowa with the State of Iowa encouraging counties, cities and towns to become designated HBI Communities to demonstrate their commitment to welcoming veterans and their families. Through discussions among local veteran organizations, city staff, and the Mayor, it was determined to be in the best interest of the City to support the HBI initiative and encourage local participation. Mayor Cavanaugh invited private individuals. Don Richardson to address and brief the Council on the initiative. This resolution declares the Council’s support for the HBI initiative and commitment to becoming a designated HBI Community, encourages local businesses, organizations, and residents to participate in HBI efforts, including offering incentives and employment opportunities for veterans and authorizes staff to work with Iowa Workforce Development and HBI to complete the designation process and promote veteran-friendly initiativesCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

      • Vote 71. Resolution Approving an MOU with EmberGrove Hospitality. Motion: Moved by Olejniczak, seconded by Shelly approving an MOU with EmberGrove Hospitality. The City of Perry and EmberGrove Hospitality have been in discussions regarding a potential lease for the operation, maintenance, and hospitality management of the Hotel Pattee and La Poste properties. A Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) was prepared outlining proposed principal terms, brand protection, and transition, and included conditions to a final lease, all being non-binding expressions of present intent. Proposed financial terms included an initial monthly base rent of $1,500 paid monthly, with annual variable rent structured at 10% for Net Income from Operations between $0 and $75,000 and 15% for income exceeding $75,000. The precise calculation, allowable expenses, later-year base rent, and any FF&E fee or replacement reserve remained subject to further discussion and agreement. This resolution authorizes the Mayor to sign the MOU between the City of Perry and EmberGrove Hospitality, acknowledging the preliminary proposed terms, while confirming that execution of the MOU does not constitute approval or execution of a final lease, nor does it bind the City to enter into a final agreement without subsequent formal Council action and approvalCarried unanimously · Count only · $75,000 · Minutes · Full page →

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 81. Resolution Authorizing the Purchase of Equipment from PHI. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Olejniczak authorizing the purchase of equipment from Perry Hospitality, Inc. (PHI). PHI requested that the City, as owner of the Hotel Pattee and La Poste properties, purchase certain furniture, fixtures, and operational equipment situated within and utilized for ongoing operations at the conclusion of their lease terms. The City’s legal counsel met with representatives of PHI to discuss terms for the purchase of said equipment, resulting in a proposed purchase price of $90,000, payable in two installments. This resolution authorizes the purchase of equipment as listed and included with the resolution, with $40,000 due upon expiration of the current lease on November 30, 2026, funded from the Local Option Sales Tax Fund balance, and the remaining $50,000 due July 15, 2027, which is to be allocated and determined during the FY2028 budgeting processPassed 4–1 · No: Mahler · $90,000 · Minutes, p. 6 · Full page →
