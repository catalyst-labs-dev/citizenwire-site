      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. City of Perry. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Andorf approving the site plan for the Jaybird Substation by Guthrie County REC. This resolution approves the Site Plan submitted by Guthrie County REC for the proposed electrical substation located on Parcel 26-68 legally described in the resolution. The Planning and Zoning Commission met on August 11, 2026, and after review recommended approval of the plan. This resolution approves the Site Plan for the electrical substation, Jaybird Substation, and states that all applicable and required regulations of the City of Perry be complied with. Furthermore, the resolution provides written consent to allow for the use of barbed wire fencing to enclose the facility”.
