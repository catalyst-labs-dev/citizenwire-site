      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Resolution Approving Task Order #14, Scope of Work for the 1,500’ Parallel Taxiway Extension. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Andorf approving Task Order #14, Scope of Work for the 1,500’ Parallel Taxiway Extension. The FAA notified the Perry Municipal Airport on February 24, 2026, of they concurrence to the proposed Task Order #14 for the Scope of Work for the 1,500’ Parallel Taxiway Extension project at the Perry Municipal Airport for the Design and Construction Engineering Services. This Resolution approves Task Order #14 in the amount of $366,800, which will be funded with 95% FAA funding under an Airport Improvement Plan (AIP) grant, with 5% local funding, totaling $18,340.00”.
