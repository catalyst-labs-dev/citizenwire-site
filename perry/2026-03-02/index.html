      • Vote 41. Resolution Terminating Development Agreement with KM Baxter Investments, LLC. Motion: Moved by Klein, seconded by Shelly approving to terminate a Development Agreement with KM Baxter Investments, LLC. A letter dated January 22, 2026, was delivered to KM Baxter Investments, LLC providing details of the Development Agreement that they entered into with the City of Perry dated May 18, 2022, and the required actions that were to take place under the terms of such Agreement. The letter requested that the Company provide evidence to the City by February 23, 2026, demonstrating the Company’s intent and ability to cure the breach in contract, to which the Company provided no evidence to the City demonstrating that the Company intended to cure the breach of contract. Therefore, the City determined that the Development Agreement should be terminated, and it was necessary for the City Council to take formal action to terminate the Development Agreement. This resolution approves the termination of the Development Agreement with KM Baxter Investments, LLCCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Development agreement. A contract between the city and a developer that spells out what each side will do. It may include required building value, deadlines, public improvements or incentives.
