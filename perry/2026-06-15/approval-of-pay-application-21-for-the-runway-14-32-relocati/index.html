      • Perry City Council

      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Approval of Pay Application #21 for the Runway 14/32 Relocation Phase 3. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Wolling approving Pay Application #21 in the amount of $102,679.82 to Manatt’s Inc. Pay Application #21 covered the increased costs of concrete pavement and cement treated subgrade associated with Phase 3 of the project sliding to a later construction season, due to the delay in obtaining the wetland mitigation credits. Pay Application #21 was reviewed and signed by the project manager, which recommended approval”. Why this has a full page: $100,000 or more.

      What this means
      Pay application
      A contractor’s request to be paid for work finished so far on an approved project. Cities often hold back a share, called retainage, until the job is complete.
