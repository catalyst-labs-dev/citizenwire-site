      Approval of Change Order #2 for the Parallel Taxiway Reconstruction

      • Perry City Council
      • Jun 15, 2026
      • Taxes and fees
      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Approval of Change Order #2 for the Parallel Taxiway Reconstruction. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Mahler approving Change Order #2 for the Parallel Taxiway Reconstruction project for a net increase of $46,465.25. Change Order #2 was to change the width of the existing taxilane for an additional distance in order to reach the apron, improving safety in aircraft movement. The FAA reviewed and approved this change order for funding under the grant and recommended proceeding with the work per their written notice dated June 11, 2024. This action approved Change Order Number 2 for a net increase of $46,465.25”. Why this has a full page: taxes or fees.

      An approved change to a construction contract the city already signed. It can raise or lower the price, or change the work or the schedule.
