      • Vote 31. Resolution Approving the Award for the Soumas Avenue Improvements Project. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Wolling approving the award of the Soumas Avenue Improvements Project to McAninch Corporation. Bids were received for the Soumas Avenue Improvements project at 2:00p.m. on June 23rd. Eight bids were received, and seven of the bidders were responsive. The Engineer’s Estimate for the base bid of the project was $3,709,836.00. Bids are summarized below: Bidder Total Base Bid McAninch Corporation $3,558,210.11 Concrete Technologies $3,742,952.00 Inc. Absolute Group $3,885,622.95 Alliance Paving LLC $3,890,987.00 TK Concrete Inc. $3,862,590.00 Reilly Construction Co. $4,129,684.00 Inc. Bolton & Menk, Inc. reviewed the bid documents submitted and checked references on this bidder and was satisfied with the responses given. Bolton & Menk recommended the award of the Soumas Avenue Improvements project to McAninch Corporation of Des Moines for a total amount of $3,558,210.11.       • Vote 101. Resolution Authorizing FY2026 Operational Transfers of Funds. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Wolling authorizing the FY2026 Operational transfers of funds. Per Legislation passed in 2019, a resolution must be completed for all transfers between funds and must include a clear statement of reason or purpose for the transfer, the name of the fund from which the transfer is originating, the name of the fund into which the transfer is to be received, and the amount to be transferred.
