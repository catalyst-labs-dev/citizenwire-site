      Contingent Upon the Awarding of a CDBG Grant from IEDA for the Roof Repair Program

      • Perry City Council
      • May 4, 2026
      • Taxes and fees
      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Contingent Upon the Awarding of a CDBG Grant from IEDA for the Roof Repair Program. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Wolling approving contracting with Region XII to provide administrative services for a CDBG Grant from IEDA for the Roof Repair Program, contingent upon an award. The City of Perry and Region XII have been working to secure a CDBG Roof Repair Program grant through IEDA. This action authorizes an Administrative Services Contract for Region XII to serve as the grant administrator, in the amount of $18,000, contingent upon the grant award. If the grant is awarded the fee will be covered under the grant”. Why this has a full page: taxes or fees.

      Background, staff memo and alternatives: Not in the minutes. See the Agenda packet: Not in the record.
