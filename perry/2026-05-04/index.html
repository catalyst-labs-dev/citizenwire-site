      • Vote 31. Resolution Approving an Application for a CDBG Grant from IEDA for the Roof Repair Program. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Andorf approving an application for a CDBG Grant from IEDA for the Roof Repair Program. The City of Perry and Region XII have been working to secure a CDBG Roof Repair Program grant through IEDA. The grant would be for 6 projects with a maximum match of $999 per project to assist low to moderate income (LMI) homeowners to improve the outside envelope of their home by providing funds to replace roof, fascia, soffit and gutter systems within the City. This action approves the application, including the 6 projects and local match, and authorizes the Mayor to sign all necessary documents for said application as detailed in the resolutionCarried unanimously · Count only · $999 · Minutes

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
