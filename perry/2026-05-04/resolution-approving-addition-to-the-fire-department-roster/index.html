      Resolution Approving Addition to the Fire Department Roster

      Perry City Council · May 4, 2026 · Public safety · City staff

      Recorded in the minutes as “1. Resolution Approving Addition to the Fire Department Roster. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Klein approving the addition of an applicant to the Fire Department Roster. Fire Chief, Grason Hill, recommended…”. Carried unanimously; names not recorded.

      What this means (Resolution): A formal council decision, usually made in one vote.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Resolution Approving Addition to the Fire Department Roster. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Klein approving the addition of an applicant to the Fire Department Roster. Fire Chief, Grason Hill, recommended approval of an applicant application to the Volunteer Fire Department. Chief Hill stated that Mr. Jans was interviewed by the Officers of the Department, and it was believed he would make a great addition. It was also stated that the Department roster was nowhere near its capacity, therefore allowing for the addition of new personnel”.

      Motion as recorded: “Moved by Shelly, seconded by Klein approving the addition of an applicant to the Fire Department Roster.
