      • Vote 61. Resolution Approving the Implementation for a New Hire Pre-Employment Screening Process. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Mahler approving the implementation of a new hire pre-employment screening process. The Safety Committee, along with TrueNorth, has been working on implementing a new hire pre-employment screening process to be conducted at Dallas County Hospital. Following acceptance of a position, new hires will complete a pre-employment medical history questionnaire, physical examination and drug screening. Such pre-employment screening is needed to mitigate future liability issues and ensure hires are able to fulfill job requirements. This resolution approves the implementation of the process and authorizes questionnaire, exam, and screening for all new hires. Legal counsel reviewed documents, including the medical history questionnaire and the process overviewCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
