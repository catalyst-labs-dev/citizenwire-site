      • Vote 61. Trust 457(b) Deferred Comp Plan. Motion: Moved by Klein, seconded by Wolling approving the MAP Retirement Plan Design and Administration Proposal. The Police Department's 457(b) deferred comp plan (social security replacement) requires a third-party administrator for plan management, reporting, compliance and design services. The City contracted with Futureplan to provide these services but after delays in communication and employee service requests, Finance Director, an applicant, requested to change the third-party administrator to MAP Retirement Services. MAP is a local provider with an office in West Des Moines whose services would cost approximately $600 less per year than the contracted. The Futureplan contract needed to be cancelled by the end of the year, therefore Council participated in a phone poll on December 24, 2025, to approve the change in Administration of the plan, which was carried to approve by a four to zero vote by the Council. This resolution formally approves the cancellation of the contract with Futureplan and approves the MAP Retirement Plan Design and Administration proposal for both the Police Trust 457(b), and the 457(b) for the City Administrator with employer match and other employees that wish to contribute without an employer matchCarried unanimously · Count only · $600 · Minutes · Full page →

        What this means: Tabling. Setting an item aside for now. A tabled item can be brought back at a later meeting. It is not a final yes or no.

      • Vote 121. Project. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Andorf approving a quotation with Municipal Pipe Tool for the 2026 CIPP Sanitary Sewer Project. This Quotation is for Cured In-Place Pipe (CIPP) lining of approximately 5,000 linear feet of existing 8-inch sanitary sewer lines. Through previous phases of sanitary sewer cleaning Staff has identified the areas shown on the map attached to the quote from Municipal Pipe Tool Co, LLC as needing CIPP lining. Staff wished to do a multi-phase approach addressing areas of town that would benefit from the CIPP lining most. This lining would be to maintain integrity of the sanitary sewer piping while eliminating Inflow and Infiltration (I&I) on the overall system. This project also involves the grouting of all residential laterals in the areas shown on the map. This resolution approves the Quotation with Municipal Pipe Tool for the 2026 CIPP Sanitary Sewer Project with an estimated cost not to exceed $272,010.00, per the Professional Services Agreement for CIPP Sewer Maintenance ServicesCarried unanimously · Count only · $272,010 · Minutes · Full page →
