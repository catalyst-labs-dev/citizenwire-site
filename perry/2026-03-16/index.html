      • Vote 31. Resolution Approving a Temporary Construction Easement. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Klein approving a Temporary Construction Easement for the Downtown Improvements Project Phase 1. Staff deemed it necessary to have a temporary construction easement for the Downtown Improvements Project Phase 1 on property owned by Sheffer Enterprises LLC. Such temporary construction easement will grant access to this area during construction and was agreed to and signed by Sheffer Enterprises LLC. This area is legally described in the agreement and resolutionCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Easement. A right for the city or a utility to use part of someone’s land for a set purpose, such as pipes or access. The owner keeps the land.

      • Vote 41. Approval of Appointments to various Boards/Commissions. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Wolling making various appointments to Boards/Commissions. an applicant submitted a request to be appointed to the Airport Commission for a four-year term which will end on 03/17/2030, and an applicant submitted a request to be appointed to the Park and Recreation Advisory Board for a three-year term which will end on 03/17/2029.       • Vote 51. Resolution Approving the Fiscal Year 2025 Annual Audit Report. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Wolling approving the Fiscal Year 2025 Annual Audit Report. Iowa State Law requires that cities do an annual audit to comply with A-133 audit requirements and due to the funding received from State or Federally funded projects. This resolution approves the FY2025 Annual Audit Report for the City of Perry as reported by the Auditor’s Office of the State of Iowa and directs City staff to submit the report as requiredCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 61. Resolution Approving a Technical Services Agreement with Region XII with Contingencies. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Mahler approving a Technical Services Agreement for a Community Development Block Grant, Roof Repair Program, contingent upon opening of such Program. Staff and Region XII representatives identified a need to assist low to moderate income (LMI) homeowners to improve the outside envelope of their home by providing funds to replace roof, fascia, soffit and gutter systems within the City, and found that this type of program could be possible through the Iowa Economic Development Authority (IEDA), Community Development Block Grant, Roof Repair Program. IEDA had yet to open such Program, but had released a tentative release date of March 18, 2026, through May 22, 2026, as the application period, with award announcements expected in the Fall of 2026. This resolution approves a Technical Services Agreement with Region XII, contingent upon IEDA opening applications, at a cost of $1,000, which includes preparation of the Environmental Review, Affirmative Fair Housing marketing, and income verifications, as required to submit an application, and the submission of an application to IEDA for the Community Development Block Grant, Roof Repair ProgramCarried unanimously · Count only · $1,000 · Minutes

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 71. Resolution Approving a Professional Services Agreement for Sanitary Flow Monitoring. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Mahler approving a Professional Servies Agreement for the Sanitary Flow Monitoring. Bolton & Menk submitted a Professional Services Agreement to provide professional services to capture the hydraulic flow and to identify areas of high Infiltration and Inflow (I&I) of the existing sanitary pipe network, focusing on the East Trunk leading to and within Zone 4 of the City’s sanitary sewer network. By monitoring the sanitary sewer system's flow, staff can then investigate the I&I areas that cause the system to surcharge and potentially back up into residential homes. This resolution approves the Professional Services Agreement in the amount not to exceed $45,000 without prior approval as per the 2026 Bolton & Menk rate scheduleCarried unanimously · Count only · $45,000 · Minutes
