      • Vote 61. Resolution Authorizing the Signing and Acceptance of a Safe Streets and Roads For All (SS4A) Grant. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Wolling authorizing and accepting the Safe Streets and Roads For All (SS4A) Grant. The City submitted an application to the U.S. Department of Transportation Federal Highway Administration for funding through a SS4A Grant for community-wide planning for a comprehensive safety action plan per resolution 063023M. Starting in March of 2024, the City worked with the Federal Highway Administration to create a grant agreement template for funding, which received approval of funding not to exceed $572,520 for the action plan, with a local match of $143,130, which will be included in the FY2028 budget through Local Option Sales Tax funding, for a total eligible project cost of $715,650. This resolution accepts the Federal Award Number 693JJ32640703 from the U.S. Department of Transportation Federal Highway Administration for community-wide planning of a comprehensive safety action plan and authorizes the City Administrator to sign all forms necessary to execute such fundingCarried unanimously · Count only · $715,650 · Minutes · Full page →

        What this means: Budget. The city’s spending plan for a fiscal year, which runs July 1 to June 30 in Iowa. Adopting it sets the property tax levy and needs a public hearing.

      • Vote 71. Resolution Approving an Urban Renewal Loan Application. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Wolling approving an Urban Renewal Loan to Mandarin Café owner an applicant, as the owner of the building located at 1117 2nd Street. This resolution provides for a $12,000 loan to be made from the City’s Urban Renewal Revolving Fund to Mandarin Café owner an applicant, as the owner of the building located at 1117 2nd Street, for the purpose of HVAC improvements, including the installation of a new Lennox furnace, indoor coil and Lennox air conditioner. This resolution also authorizes the development of a loan agreement and security instruments necessary to carry out the terms of the loan as well as directs staff to execute the necessary documents and make payments for eligible costs. The $12,000 loan amount will carry a 3% interest rate, amortized over 5 years with 60 monthly paymentsCarried unanimously · Count only · $12,000 · Minutes

        What this means: Tax increment financing (TIF). Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.
