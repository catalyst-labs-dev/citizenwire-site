      • Vote 31. Resolution Approving Requests for Grant Funding for a K9 and Officer Training and Authorizing related K9 Program Expenses. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Wolling approving the requests for grant funding for the K9 and Officer Training programs and authorizing related K9 program expenses. The Perry Police Department has actively been inquiring on obtaining a K9 for the Department and presented to the Council on how bringing such a program back would provide a valuable resource in their mission to protect officers, reduce crime, and better serve the citizens through a professionally managed K9 program. Council, through the FY2027 budget process, approved capital funds for such K9 program and the Department has informally secured funding for a K9, along with the required training necessary for a K9 Officer. This resolution approves the formal requests be submitted to the Oz Grant for a K9, in the amount of $12,000 and the USPCA Grant for the K9 Officer training, in the amount of $7,500 along with authorizing the local funds, as approved with the FY2027 budget, of $15,000 for necessary K9 program expenses. The resolution also acknowledges the fundraising strategies of the Perry Police Officers Association for the K9 program both in implementation of the program and in the future for program expenditures, authorizes a selected Officer from the Perry Police Department, at the discretion of the Police Chief, attend the necessary K9 Officer training in Minnesota that runs 10-12 weeks starting August 03, 2026, upon receipt of grant funds and names the K9 Ozzy – Groot AKA GrootCarried unanimously · Count only · $15,000 · Minutes · Full page →

        What this means: Budget. The city’s spending plan for a fiscal year, which runs July 1 to June 30 in Iowa. Adopting it sets the property tax levy and needs a public hearing.

      • Vote 91. Resolution Consenting to Assignment of Development Agreement and Tax Increment Payments. Motion: Moved by Mahler, seconded by Klein consenting to the assignment of a Development Agreement and Tax Increment Payments. The City Council approved a Development Agreement with Semper Fi Commercial Solutions, LLC on May 16, 2022, per resolution 051622D, which authorized annual appropriation tax increment payments and pledged certain tax increment revenues to the payment of the Agreement. Semper Fi Commercial Solutions, LLC was to construct a commercial building within the Perry Industrial Park to provide space rentable for contractors and other commercial or industrial users. Due to unforeseen circumstances, the construction did not take place, therefore missing key deadlines within the original Development Agreement. Semper Fi wrote to the City on June 23, 2023, requesting an amendment with extended deadlines. Such amendment was granted through an Amended Development Agreement dated August 8th, 2023, per resolution 080723F. Semper Fi again requested another amendment take place to extend the timing requirements within the Agreement, which such amendment was granted through the Second Amended Development Agreement dated August 20th, 2024, per resolution 081924A. Semper Fi is now requesting consent to the assignment of all of its rights and obligations under the Second Amended Development Agreement to 141 Properties, LLC, whom will undertake the construction of an industrial building for leasing by and use in the business operations of commercial/industrial enterprises on the property. This resolution allows for the consent to Semper Fi’s request for assignment to 141 Properties, LLC, and accepts and authorizes the signing of the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Third Amended Development Agreement, and the Minimum Assessment AgreementCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes · Full page →

        What this means: Tax increment financing (TIF). Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.
