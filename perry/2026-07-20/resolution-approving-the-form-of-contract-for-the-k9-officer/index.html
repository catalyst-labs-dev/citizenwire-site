      Resolution Approving the Form of Contract for the K9 Officer Service Agreement

      Perry City Council · Jul 20, 2026 · Public safety

      Recorded in the minutes as “1. Resolution Approving the Form of Contract for the K9 Officer Service Agreement. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Klein approving the form of contract for the K9 Officer Service Agreement. With the revival of the…”. Carried unanimously; names not recorded.

      What this means (Resolution): A formal council decision, usually made in one vote.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Resolution Approving the Form of Contract for the K9 Officer Service Agreement. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Klein approving the form of contract for the K9 Officer Service Agreement. With the revival of the K9 Program within the Perry Police Department it is imperative that a K9 Officer Service Agreement be approved for use by the Police Department. This resolution approves the K9 Officer Service Agreement, as reviewed by legal counsel, and authorizes that the K9 Officer be selected at the discretion of the Police Chief”.

      Motion as recorded: “Moved by Wolling, seconded by Klein approving the form of contract for the K9 Officer Service Agreement. With the revival of the K9 Program within the Perry Police Department it is imperative that a K9 Officer Service Agreement be approved for use by the Police Department. This resolution approves the K9 Officer Service Agreement, as reviewed by legal counsel, and authorizes that the K9 Officer be selected at the discretion of the Police Chief”.
