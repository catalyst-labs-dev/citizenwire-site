      Transportation Airport Funding and Certifying Eligibility Requirements

      • Perry City Council
      • Apr 20, 2026
      • Taxes and fees
      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “1. Transportation Airport Funding and Certifying Eligibility Requirements. Motion: Moved by Wolling, seconded by Mahler authorizing the application submission for the Iowa Department of Transportation Airport Funding and certifying eligibility requirements. The State of Iowa takes grant requests annually for consideration for state aviation funds. This grant application includes a request from the Airport Improvement Program (AIP) construction of general aviation hangar taxilanes at the Perry Municipal Airport. If the Construct GA Hangar Taxilanes improvement project is selected by the Iowa DOT for funding, a grant would pay 80% of the project cost and the remaining 20% shall be paid from local funds. The Airport Commission met on April 13, 2026, and provided recommendation for such grant application. This resolution approves the submission of such grant application and certifies the eligibility requirements, including the local share amount of $100,000”. Why this has a full page: $100,000 or more, taxes or fees.
