      • Vote 41. Resolution Authorizing the Signing and Acceptance of the Revitalize Iowa’s Sound Economy (RISE) Program Funding. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Wolling authorizing the signing and acceptance of the Revitalize Iowa’s Sound Economy (RISE) Program Funding for the Soumas Avenue project and the Willis Avenue/Highway 141 Roundabout. The City submitted an application to the Iowa Department of Transportation for funding through the Revitalize Iowa’s Sound Economy (RISE) program for the Soumas Avenue project and the Willis Avenue/Highway 141 Roundabout project per Resolutions 081825B, 090225E and 040626B, and on May 22, 2025, the City of Perry was notified by the DOT per the Transportation Commission Order No TD-2026-87 on May 12, 2026, that the City of Perry was approved for funding through the RISE program. Such RISE funding is for $2,894,620 of Immediate Opportunity RISE funds for the Soumas Avenue project and $2,626,162 of Local Development RISE funds for the Willis Avenue/Highway 141 Roundabout project, with a local contribution of $2,474,431 setting the project totals at $7,995,213, plus all engineering costs. This resolution accepts the Iowa DOT Agreement for RISE funding 2026-R-001 for Project No. RM-5970(620)- -9D-25 from the Iowa Department of Transportation (DOT) for the Revitalize Iowa’s Sound Economy (RISE) program, authorizes the local contribution, along with the Mayor’s signature on all forms necessary to execute such agreementCarried unanimously · Count only · $7,995,213 · Minutes · Full page →

      • Vote 71. Resolution Approving the Amended Development Agreement with JC & JC Homes, LLC. Motion: Moved by Shelly, seconded by Klein approving an Amended Development Agreement with JC & JC Homes, LLC. The City Council approved a Development Agreement with JC & JC Homes, LLC on April 22, 2025, per resolution 042125B, to undertake and complete whatever necessary physical improvements to maintain the property located at 1918 W 4th Street, as set forth in its RFP proposal documents. JC & JC Homes, LLC requested an amendment take place to extend the timing requirements within the Agreement to December 31, 2026, due to unforeseen delays, however completion is anticipated to occur prior per the construction timeline provided within the extension request letter. This resolution approves the Amended Development Agreement including the extension request for project completion from August 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026Carried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Development agreement. A contract between the city and a developer that spells out what each side will do. It may include required building value, deadlines, public improvements or incentives.

      • Vote 81. Resolution Approving a Permanent Public Utility Easement and Ingress/Egress Easement. Motion: Moved by Klein, seconded by Mahler approving a Permanent Public Utility Easement and Ingress/Egress Easement with Wells Fargo Bank. It was been deemed necessary to have a permanent public utility easement along with an ingress/egress easement on property owned by Wells Fargo Bank for utility infrastructure being installed for the Downtown Improvements Phase I Project. Wells Fargo Bank agreed to and signed the easement document. The easement areas were legally described in the agreement and resolution. This resolution approves such a permanent easement and ingress/egress easement and authorizes the fully executed agreement to be sent to the County Recorder for filingCarried unanimously · Count only · Minutes

        What this means: Easement. A right for the city or a utility to use part of someone’s land for a set purpose, such as pipes or access. The owner keeps the land.

      • Vote 91. Resolution Approving an Encroachment Agreement. Motion: Moved by Andorf, seconded by Mahler approving an Encroachment Agreement. After staff review and consideration of the utility it was determined that an encroachment agreement could allow for their request.
