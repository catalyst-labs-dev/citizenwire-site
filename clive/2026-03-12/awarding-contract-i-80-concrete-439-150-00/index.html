      Awarding Contract - I-80 Concrete - $439,150.00

      Clive City Council · Mar 12, 2026 · City government

      Recorded in the minutes as “c. Resolution 2026-072 Awarding Contract - I-80 Concrete - $439,150.00. Motion: to adopt the resolution awarding the contract”. Passed 4–0; Yes: Weaver, Klein, Hocker, Gasparotto. Amount in the record: $439,150.

      What this means (Bids and contracts): For public construction, the city approves plans and a cost estimate, takes bids, then awards the contract, usually to the lowest responsible bidder.
