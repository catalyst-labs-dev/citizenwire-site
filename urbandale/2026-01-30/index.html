      • Vote 12. APPROVE AGENDA Motion: Motion by Councilmember Carberry Montgomery, second by Councilmember Croll to approve the January 29, 2026 agendaPassed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 1

        What this means: Approving the agenda. A routine first vote that sets the list of items for the meeting.

      • Vote 21. Metro Ice Facility - Establish Public Hearing to Convey Real Property and Consideration of a Short-Term Use Agreement (5100 72nd Street) a property owner outlined the current lease arrangement and the proposed short- term use and purchase agreements. Motion: Motion by Councilmember Rozendaal, second by Councilmember Boddy to approve Resolution 066-2026: Establishing March 3, 2026, as a public hearing for the consideration of a proposal to convey a portion of real property to Sig Sportsplex, LLC (pertaining to Metro Ice Facility) and providing for publication of notice thereofPassed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 1

        What this means: Public hearing. A set time at a meeting when anyone can speak to the council about a proposal before it decides. Hearings are required by law for some actions, such as budgets, rezoning and borrowing.

      • Vote 3Motion by Councilmember Rozendaal, second by Councilmember Boddy to approve Resolution 067-2026: Authorizing execution of a short-term use agreement, subject to receipt of the required liability insurance certificate by end of business today, and authorizing the Mayor to sign the agreementPassed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 2

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
