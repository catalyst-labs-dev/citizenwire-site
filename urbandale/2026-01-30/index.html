Motion: Motion by Councilmember Rozendaal, second by Councilmember Boddy to approve Resolution 066-2026: Establishing March 3, 2026, as a public hearing for the consideration of a proposal to convey a portion of real property to Sig Sportsplex, LLC (pertaining to Metro Ice Facility) and providing for publication of notice thereofPassed 5–0 · Minutes
        What this means

        Public hearing: A set time at a meeting when anyone can speak to the council about a proposal before it decides. Hearings are required by law for some actions, such as budgets, rezoning and borrowing.

      • Vote 3Motion by Councilmember Rozendaal, second by Councilmember Boddy to approve Resolution 067-2026: Authorizing execution of a short-term use agreement, subject to receipt of the required liability insurance certificate by end of business today, and authorizing the Mayor to sign the agreementPassed 5–0 · Minutes
        What this means

        Resolution: A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
