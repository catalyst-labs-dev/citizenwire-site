      Bids - 86th Street Trail Project

      Urbandale City Council · Apr 7, 2026 · Getting around

      Recorded in the minutes as “4. Bids - 86th Street Trail Project Motion: Motion by Councilmember Croll, second by Councilmember Carberry Montgomery to approve Resolution 124-2026: Making award of contract to Howrey Construction, Inc. in the amount…”. Passed 5–0; Yes: Boddy, Cacciatore, Montgomery, Croll, Rozendaal. Amount in the record: $180,458.

      What this means (Bids and contracts): For public construction, the city approves plans and a cost estimate, takes bids, then awards the contract, usually to the lowest responsible bidder.
