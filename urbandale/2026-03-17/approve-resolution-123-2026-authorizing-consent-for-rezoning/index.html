      Approve Resolution 123-2026: Authorizing Consent for Rezoning of Property at 5100 72nd Street

      Urbandale City Council · Mar 17, 2026 · Zoning

      Recorded in the minutes as “Approve Resolution 123-2026: Authorizing Consent for Rezoning of Property at 5100 72nd Street”. Passed 5–0 as part of the consent agenda; Yes: Boddy, Cacciatore, Montgomery, Croll, Rozendaal.
