      Establish Public Hearing - Proposed Property Tax Levy

      Urbandale City Council · Feb 17, 2026 · Taxes and fees

      Recorded in the minutes as “3. Establish Public Hearing - Proposed Property Tax Levy Motion: Motion by Councilmember Rozendaal, second by Councilmember Carberry Montgomery to approve Resolution 084-2026: Establishing April 7, 2026, as a public…”. Passed 5–0; Yes: Boddy, Cacciatore, Montgomery, Croll, Rozendaal. The item included a public hearing.

      What this means (Public hearing): A set time at a meeting when anyone can speak to the council about a proposal before it decides.
