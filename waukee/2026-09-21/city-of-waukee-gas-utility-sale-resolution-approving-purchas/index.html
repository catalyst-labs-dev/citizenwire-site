      What this means
      Plat
      A map that divides land into lots, streets and easements. A preliminary plat is the proposed layout. A final plat is the official version that gets recorded with the county.
      Change order
      An approved change to a construction contract the city already signed. It can raise or lower the price, or change the work or the schedule.
