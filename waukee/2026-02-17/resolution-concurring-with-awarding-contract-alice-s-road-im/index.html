      Resolution concurring with awarding contract: Alice’s Road Improvements Phase 1B Project

      Waukee City Council · Feb 17, 2026 · Budget · Getting around

      Recorded in the minutes as “Alice’s Road Improvements Phase 1B Project – B. Resolution: Consideration of approval of a resolution concurring with awarding contract – Motion: to approve the resolution; seconded by Council Member Crone. Public Works Director/City Engineer Koester reviewed the bid tabulation, noting that the apparent low…”. Passed 5–0; Yes: Anderson, Crone, Grove, Lyon, Pierce. Amount in the record: $2,611,078.91.

      What this means (Bids and contracts): For public construction, the city approves plans and a cost estimate, takes bids, then awards the contract, usually to the lowest responsible bidder.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “Alice’s Road Improvements Phase 1B Project – B. Resolution: Consideration of approval of a resolution concurring with awarding contract – Motion: to approve the resolution; seconded by Council Member Crone. Public Works Director/City Engineer Koester reviewed the bid tabulation, noting that the apparent low bid was approximately 10% over the project engineer’s estimate; however, the Phase 1A bid had been below the estimate so the total of both projects remained under budget. He recommended concurring with the City of Clive award of contract to Mainline Construction of Des Moines, IA, in the amount of $2,611,078.91. Mayor Clarke asked if the contractor had contributed to Phase 1A construction delays; Mr. Koester replied in the affirmative and reviewed some of the issues with Phase 1A construction. Council Member Pierce asked about the City’s role since it was Clive’s contract; Mr. Koester discussed how Waukee and Clive collaborated on joint construction projects. Results of vote:”.
