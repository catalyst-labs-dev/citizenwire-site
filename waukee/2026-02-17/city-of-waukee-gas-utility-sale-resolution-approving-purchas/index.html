      What this means
      Setting a hearing
      This kind of vote schedules a public hearing for a later meeting and allows notice to be published. It does not approve the proposal itself.
      Change order
      An approved change to a construction contract the city already signed. It can raise or lower the price, or change the work or the schedule.
