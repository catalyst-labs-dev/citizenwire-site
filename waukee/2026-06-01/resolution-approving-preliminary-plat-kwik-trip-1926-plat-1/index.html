      Resolution approving preliminary plat: Kwik Trip 1926 Plat 1/Kwik Star #1926

      • Waukee City Council
      • Jun 1, 2026
      • Zoning · Getting around
      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “Kwik Trip 1926 Plat 1/Kwik Star #1926 – A. Resolution: Consideration of approval of a resolution approving preliminary plat – Motion: to approve the resolution; seconded by Council Member Grove. Planning Coordinator DeBoer presented the preliminary plat, final plat and site plan for a convenience store/gas station with fuel canopy. She noted there was no car wash planned for the site. She then reviewed site ingress/egress, recreational trails and sidewalks, parking, landscape buffer requirements, public utilities, stormwater detention basins, and exterior building elevations. Council Member Lyon asked why there was no car wash as part of the plans; Ms. DeBoer answered that the required stormwater detention took up space that was originally intended for a car wash. Results of vote:”. Why this has a full page: zoning.

      What this means
      Plat
      A map that divides land into lots, streets and easements. A preliminary plat is the proposed layout. A final plat is the official version that gets recorded with the county.
      Site plan
      A detailed drawing of how one property will be built, such as buildings, parking, driveways and landscaping. The city checks it against its rules.
