      What this is about

      Item 3 of the consent agenda, recorded in the minutes as: “Consideration of approval of a motion setting July 6, 2026, as the date of public hearing on an ordinance to amend Chapter 169, Zoning Ordinance, City of Waukee, Iowa, by rezoning certain property from K-MF-SM [Kettlestone Multi-Family Stacked Medium District] to K-MF-SM/PD-1 [Kettlestone Multi-Family Stacked Medium District with a Planned Development Overlay] (The Wren)”. The council approved the whole consent agenda in one vote. Why this has a full page: zoning.

      What this means
      Public hearing
      A set time at a meeting when anyone can speak to the council about a proposal before it decides. Hearings are required by law for some actions, such as budgets, rezoning and borrowing.
      Rezoning
      Zoning sets what can be built or done on each piece of land, such as homes, shops or industry. Changing it is done by ordinance, usually after a planning commission review and a public hearing.

      A general definition of this kind of action, not a statement about this item.
