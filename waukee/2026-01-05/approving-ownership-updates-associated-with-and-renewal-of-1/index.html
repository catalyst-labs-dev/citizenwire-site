      What this means
      Development agreement
      A contract between the city and a developer that spells out what each side will do. It may include required building value, deadlines, public improvements or incentives.
      Plat
      A map that divides land into lots, streets and easements. A preliminary plat is the proposed layout. A final plat is the official version that gets recorded with the county.
