        What this means: Consent agenda. A list of routine items the council approves together in one vote, without separate discussion. Any member can ask to pull an item out for its own vote.

      • Vote 92) Mayor’s Appointment and Resolution: Consideration of approval of a resolution approving mayor’s appointment or reappointment of citizen members to the various City boards and commissions – Mayor Clarke made the following appointments: Board of Adjustment, term ending 01/31/2031: Patrick Norton … Motion: to approve the resolution; seconded by Council Member Grove. Results of vote:Passed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 16

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 103) Ordinance: Consideration of approval of an ordinance to adopt and establish Chapter 29, Waukee Civil Rights Commission [third (final) reading] – Motion: Council Member Grove moved to approve the third reading of the ordinance in title only and place it on final passage;Results of vote:Passed 5–0 · as amended · Minutes, p. 3

        What this means: Ordinance readings. An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.

      • Vote 125) Resolution: Consideration of approval of a resolution approving final plat for Kettlestone Lakes Plat 1 – Motion: to approve the resolution; seconded by Council Member Crone. Planning Coordinator DeBoer briefly reviewed the plat. Council Member Pierce noted that concerns were raised by neighbors when this property was rezoned, asking if those concerns were resolved; City Administrator Deets stated that the concerns related to a different property but that the concerns were not yet resolved.         What this means: Rezoning. Zoning sets what can be built or done on each piece of land, such as homes, shops or industry. Changing it is done by ordinance, usually after a planning commission review and a public hearing.

      • Vote 136) Resolution: Consideration of approval of a resolution approving final plat for Painted Woods West Plat 6 – Motion: to approve the resolution; seconded by Council Member Lyon.
