      Waive the rules and allow the consideration and approval of an additional Class C Retail…

      Sioux City City Council · Jun 1, 2026 · City government

      Recorded in the minutes as “Motion by Schoenherr, second by Rayford, to waive the rules and allow the consideration and approval of an additional Class C Retail Alcohol License for The Diving Elk, 1101 4th Street (Temporary Outdoor Service)”. Passed 4–1; Yes: Berenstein, Rayford, Schoenherr, Scott. No: Bertrand.

      What this means (Alcohol and tobacco permits): Businesses need local approval before the state issues an alcohol or tobacco permit.
