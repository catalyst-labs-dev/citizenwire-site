      Adopt the proposed resolution as part of the consent agenda

      Sioux City City Council · Feb 9, 2026 · City government

      Recorded in the minutes as “to adopt the proposed resolution as part of the consent agenda”. Passed 4–1; Yes: Berenstein, Rayford, Schoenherr, Scott. No: Bertrand.

      What this means (Consent agenda): A list of routine items the council approves together in one vote, without separate discussion.
