      What this means
      Tax increment financing (TIF)
      Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.
      Resolution
      A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
