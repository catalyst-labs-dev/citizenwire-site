Know what your local government decided.

What was decided. Full stop.

City councils, county boards and school boards make choices that touch your rent, taxes, roads, and schools. Citizen Wire tells you what they decided, in plain words.

How it works

  1. We follow public meetings and official records.
  2. We write down what was decided and who voted how.
  3. We share each side’s reasons in their own words.
  4. Every fact links to its official source, so you can check it.

Our neutrality promise

We do not take sides. We do not use loaded words. We report the decision, the vote, and the reasons. You decide what you think.

Starting in Des Moines

We are starting with Des Moines and Polk County, Iowa. More cities will come later.
