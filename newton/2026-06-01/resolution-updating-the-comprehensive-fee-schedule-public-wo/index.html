      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “30. Resolution updating the Comprehensive Fee Schedule, Public Works Fees, Solid Waste and Recycling Collection and Disposal Fee Motion: Moved by Mills, seconded by Roth to adopt the Resolution. Dodd’s Trash Hauling was commended for their service to the community. Roth asked whether the City incurred additional costs from the 2020 derecho cleanup and what would happen if a similar disaster occurred again. Utilities Director Jody Rhone explained that City staff— primarily Public Works employees— collected more than $1 million in yard waste debris, with costs reimbursed through FEMA. Rhone noted that FEMA would provide assistance again if a similar natural disaster occurred”.
