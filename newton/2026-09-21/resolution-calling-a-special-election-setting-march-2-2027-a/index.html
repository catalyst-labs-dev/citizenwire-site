      Resolution calling a special election, setting March 2, 2027, as the Special Election Date and…

      Newton City Council · Sep 21, 2026 · Public safety · City staff

      Recorded in the minutes as “21. Resolution calling a special election, setting March 2, 2027, as the Special Election Date and adopting ballot language on the proposition of a new electric utility franchise agreement between the City of Newton and…”. Passed 6–0; names not recorded.

      What this means (Franchise fee): A fee a utility pays for using city streets and land for its lines.

      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “21. Resolution calling a special election, setting March 2, 2027, as the Special Election Date and adopting ballot language on the proposition of a new electric utility franchise agreement between the City of Newton and Interstate Power and Light Company Moved by Hallam, seconded by Simbro to adopt Motion: Moved by Holschuh, seconded by Simbro to amend the summary language by adding: " to be spent on repair and improvement of facilities, public safety operations and equipment, public transportation and public grounds infrastructure"”.

      Motion as recorded: “Moved by Holschuh, seconded by Simbro to amend the summary language by adding: " to be spent on repair and improvement of facilities, public safety operations and equipment, public transportation and public grounds infrastructure"”.

      The minutes give only the count (6–0), so individual votes are Not in the record. Vote line as printed: “AYES: 6. NAYS: None.”.
