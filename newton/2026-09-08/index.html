      Newton: Suspend the rules and waive the second and third consideration of the Ordinance

      Sep 8, 2026 · Vote 5–1

      What this means: Waiving readings. An ordinance normally gets three votes at separate meetings. Waiving readings skips the later votes so it can pass sooner. In Iowa that takes a larger majority than a normal vote.
