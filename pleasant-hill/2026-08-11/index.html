      • Vote 2i. Hodges/ Motion: to approve Resolution #081126-01 – Dispose and convey a portion of NE 12th Avenue in the 4900 block and the entirety of NE 52nd Court. Mayor Sara Kurovski opened the public hearing at 6:08 p.m. Brian Dunkelberger, Senior Planner, presented on the disposal of a portion of public right-of-way in the 4900 block of NE 12th Avenue and the entirety of the NE 52nd Court that has been vacated previously by City Council. Transfer to adjacent owners is the only option available. Disposal of the portion of NE 12th Avenue in the 4900 block will allow Elder Corporation to bring their existing site into conformance with minimum City Code requirements. Disposal of the NE 52nd Court right-of-way will remove a dead-end and minimally used public street out of the City’s inventory, and it will allow for greater use of the property through potential, taxable development in the future. The public hearing was closed at 6:10 p.mPassed 4–0 · Minutes, p. 1 · Full page →

        What this means: Public hearing. A set time at a meeting when anyone can speak to the council about a proposal before it decides. Hearings are required by law for some actions, such as budgets, rezoning and borrowing.

      • Vote 3i. DeMoss/ Motion: to approve the First Reading of Ordinance #974 – Vacation of Street Right-of-Way – portion of NE 12th Avenue in the 5100 block Mayor Sara Kurovski opened the public hearing at 6:11 p.m. Brian Dunkelberger, Senior Planner presented the City has received a request from Elder Corporation to vacate and dispose of a portion of public right-of-way in the 5100 block of NE 12th Avenue. The vacation of this street right-of-way would remove its status as public roadway and would allow for the separate process of disposition where the property would be considered for sale to adjacent owners. Transfer to adjacent owners is the only available option in this case as the property is not large enough to be buildable on its own. Both adjacent owners were notified prior to the public hearing. The public hearing was closed at 6:12 p.mPassed 4–0 · Minutes, p. 1

        What this means: Ordinance readings. An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.
