      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “i. Hodges/ Motion: to approve Resolution #081126-01 – Dispose and convey a portion of NE 12th Avenue in the 4900 block and the entirety of NE 52nd Court. Mayor Sara Kurovski opened the public hearing at 6:08 p.m. Brian Dunkelberger, Senior Planner, presented on the disposal of a portion of public right-of-way in the 4900 block of NE 12th Avenue and the entirety of the NE 52nd Court that has been vacated previously by City Council. Transfer to adjacent owners is the only option available. Disposal of the portion of NE 12th Avenue in the 4900 block will allow Elder Corporation to bring their existing site into conformance with minimum City Code requirements. Disposal of the NE 52nd Court right-of-way will remove a dead-end and minimally used public street out of the City’s inventory, and it will allow for greater use of the property through potential, taxable development in the future. The public hearing was closed at 6:10 p.m”. Why this has a full page: taxes or fees.

      What this means
      Public hearing
      A set time at a meeting when anyone can speak to the council about a proposal before it decides. Hearings are required by law for some actions, such as budgets, rezoning and borrowing.
      Resolution
      A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
