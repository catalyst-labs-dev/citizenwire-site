      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “e. Resolution #081126-07 – Approving Updated Mitigation Strategies for the Polk County Hazard Mitigation Plan DeMoss/ Motion: to approve Resolution #081126-07 – Approving Updated Mitigation Strategies for the Polk County Hazard Mitigation Plan Ben Champ, City Manager, shared that Pleasant Hill is a member of the Polk County Emergency Management Commission and participates in the Polk County Multi-Jurisdictional Local Hazard Mitigation Plan. This Plan is updated every five years with the last plan update and adoption occurring in December 2024. The following resolution updates City’s list of mitigation strategies in the plan to allow the current list of potential projects to be eligible for disaster funding”.
