      • Vote 3a. Third Reading of Ordinance #968 – Vacation of Street Right-of-Way – NE 52nd Court Murray/ Motion: to approve the Third Reading of Ordinance #968 – Vacation of Street Right-of-Way – NE 52nd Court. Assistant City Manager/Community Development Director Madeline Sturms explained that the City has received a request from Elder Corporation to vacate and dispose of the NE 52nd Court right-of-way. The vacation of this street right-of-way would remove its status as public roadway and would allow for the separate process of disposition where the property would be considered for sale to adjacent owners. Transfer to adjacent owners is the only available option in this case as the property is not large enough to be buildable on its own. Both adjacent owners were notified prior to the public hearing. There are multiple steps required in the process and two public hearings are required. The first of which is for the vacation of the subject right-of- way, and another subsequent public hearing will be scheduled for the consideration of property disposal through a purchase agreement. The City Planning and Zoning Commission considered the request at their regular meeting on March 2, 2026, and unanimously voted to recommend approval of the request. City Council then approved the first reading of the ordinance after a public hearing at their regular meeting on March 24, 2026, and the second reading was approved on April 14, 2026Passed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 1 · Full page →

        What this means: Ordinance readings. An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.

      • Vote 8f. Resolution #042826-07 – Approve Deferred Compensation Policy Konrad/ Motion: to approve Resolution #042826-07 – Approve Deferred Compensation Policy. City Manager Ben Champ explained that The City has a long-standing deferred compensation program that offers a match to full time employees of the City. In the upcoming union contract beginning July 1, 2026, a change in the City match will increase from one to two percent. The primary purpose of the attached policy is to coordinate with the new contract by adjusting the base match for the non-contract employees to two percent for parity. The policy also provides clarity for which employees are eligible for higher levels of match and establishes a grandfathering of match status for a few positions that would not be eligible upon turnover. The program would remain voluntary and continue to require a dollar-for-dollar match to reach the full employer participation level. Any employer contributions moving forward would be placed in a 401(a) plan account rather than a 457 Plan for the contribution level flexibility of the employee and the procedural benefits to the employer. The Policy changes would be effective July 1, 2026Passed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 3
