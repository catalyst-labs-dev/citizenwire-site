      Approve 2026 Springtime Hill Climb Special Event Application

      • Pleasant Hill City Council
      • Mar 10, 2026
      • Public safety · Parks
      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “c. Approve 2026 Springtime Hill Climb Special Event Application Hodges/ Motion: to approve 2026 Springtime Hill Climb Special Event Application. Parks and Recreation Manager Ryan Merritt explained that the City of Pleasant Hill Parks and Recreation Department is planning to host the 14th annual Springtime Hill Climb 5K. Event day operations will be handled by True Time Racing. The City is partnering with the Pleasant Hill Chamber of Commerce on the event. The event will be held on Saturday, April 11th, with the run starting at 8:30 AM at Doanes Park. The route will remain the same as previous years. Staff will work with public safety to review course staging and any road closures. an applicant requested any road closure information on Shadyview to be sent to her”. Why this has a full page: public safety.
