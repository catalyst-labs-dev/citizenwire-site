      • Vote 5a. Approve 2026 Farmer’s Market Special Event Application Hodges/ Motion: to approve 2026 Farmer’s Market Special Event Application. Parks and Recreation Manager Ryan Merritt explained that Berean Church is proposing their annual Farmer’s Market to be held on Mondays, June 1 through August 31 from 4-7 PM. The market will take place in the parking lot of Berean Church and is expecting 40+ vendors. The City of Pleasant Hill's Special Event Application is necessary for the use of private property, public property and/or park property that requires amplified sound and use of temporary structures (tents, canopies, stages, inflatables), including farmer’s markets. The Pleasant Hill Farmer’s Market brings urban and rural communities together, supporting local vendors from across the metro. The market offers diverse products from locally grown produce, arts and crafts, as well as food trucks. The event application has been reviewed and recommended by all necessary departmentsPassed 4–0 · Minutes
      • Vote 6b. Approve 2026 Chamber of Commerce Special Event Applications Murray/ Motion: to approve 2026 Chamber of Commerce Special Event Applications. Parks and Recreation Manager Ryan Merritt explained that the Pleasant Hill Chamber of Commerce is planning its annual event season to include, Chill on the Hill, Summer Fest, and Trick-or-Treat Around the Lake. Chill on the Hill: will be held on Fridays beginning May 29th and ending on June 26th. The event will take place from 6-9 PM. All Chill on the Hill events will be held at Copper Creek Lake Park near the shelter area for persons 21 years of age and older. Chill on the Hill consists of weekly bands, food vendors and adult beverages. Summer Fest: will be held on Friday, July 24th and Saturday, July 25th. Friday will host live entertainment, and fireworks at Copper Creek Lake Park from 6-9:30 PM and Saturday will be the parade and kid friendly activities from 12-3 PM. The start time for the parade will be 10:00 AM. Trick-or-Treat Around the Lake: will be held on Thursday, October 22nd. The activities scheduled for the day include a family-friendly walk around Copper Creek Lake, with vendor tables and Halloween themed activities. The event will set-up at 4 PM and festivities will begin at 5 PM and run until 7 PM. The event application has been reviewed and recommended by all departmentsPassed 4–0 · Minutes

      • Vote 8d. Second Reading of Ordinance #967 – Consider zoning map amendment for property generally located in the 7000 block of SE 6th Avenue from A-1 to R-2 Muray/ Motion: to approve the Second Reading of Ordinance #967 – Consider zoning map amendment for property generally located in the 7000 block of SE 6th Avenue from A-1 to R-2. Senior Planner Brain Dunkelberger explained that the City Council has the authority, in accordance with City Code Chapter 166 Procedures, Amendments, Enforcement, and Nonconformities, Section 05 Amendments, to amend, supplement, or change the zoning district designations and/or boundaries established by the zoning map after proper public notices and hearings and after receiving a recommendation from the Planning & Zoning Commission. The City received a written request to change the zoning classification of property generally located in the 7000 block of SE 6th Avenue, then passed Resolution 011326-05 on January 13, 2026, to refer the request to the Planning & Zoning Commission. The subject property is currently zoned A-1 Agricultural/Open Space and is used for row crop production. Surrounding properties include additional row crop land to the south, acreage-style single-family properties to the southwest, east, and north, and undeveloped residential land to the northwest - platted as Peter’s Ridge Subdivision in 2018. The Pleasant Hill corporate limits boundary borders most of the subject property. The Future Land Use Plan, as adopted in Pleasant Hill 2050, the City’s comprehensive plan, designates the subject property as “Low Density Residential” which is defined as single-family detached dwellings with densities ranging from 1 to 5 dwelling units per acre. City Planning and Zoning Commission considered the request at their regular meeting on February 2, 2026, and voted to recommend approval of the request. A public hearing was then set via Resolution 021026-02 at the February 10, 2026, City Council meeting, and the first reading of the ordinance was approved at the regular City Council meeting on February 24, 2026. No comments or questions have been received since the first readingPassed 4–0 · Minutes · Full page →
