      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “f. Resolution #052626-12 – Hiring side by side Firefighter Medic Position Birch/DeMoss moved to approve Resolution #052626-12 – Hiring side by side Firefighter Medic Position. Motion: t this position is temporary and can end prior to the projected end date or extended past the estimated date given. There was council member discussion. Pleasant Hill resident George Salnave said that the Fire Department and Police Department do an outstanding job and should be recognized for that”. The minutes add: “Assistant Fire Chief Ryan Hill explained that the Pleasant Hill Fire Department requests approval to fill a spot with a side-by-side full-time position. This position is needed to assist with covering the vacancy created by an employee who will be on limited duty and FMLA for the remainder of the year 2026. One employee will be unavailable to work regular shifts due to limited duty and approved FMLA leave. This creates a staffing gap that could impact operations and service continuity. Hiring a side-by-side …”

      Motion as recorded: “t this position is temporary and can end prior to the projected end date or extended past the estimated date given. There was council member discussion. Pleasant Hill resident George Salnave said that the Fire Department and Police Department do an outstanding job and should be recognized for that”.
