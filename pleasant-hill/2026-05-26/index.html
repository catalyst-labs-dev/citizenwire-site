Second Reading of Ordinance #969 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 98 Sewer Service Charges Murray/ Motion: to approve the Second Reading of Ordinance #969 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 98 Sewer Service Charges. City Manager Ben Champ explained that during the preparation of the FY27 budget it was identified that the rates for sanitary sewer service needed to be increased. The City of Pleasant Hill is a member of the Wastewater Reclamation Authority (WRA) and as a member must pay annually to the WRA for the City’s proportionate share of operation and maintenance costs and for debt service payments. The WRA has approved a new facility plan which calls for significant capital improvements and bonding in the upcoming fiscal years that will increase Pleasant Hill’s debt payments to the WRA. The rate increase proposed in this ordinance is 7.25% which will increase the base rate from $8.92 to $9.57 per 1,000 gallons and the sewer access charge from $14.96 to $16.05 per unit. That increase will impact the average residential customer by $1.74 per month. The increase will be .65 cents per 1,000 gallons of water consumed, plus an increase of $1.09 per month for the sewer access charge. The last rate increase the City approved was July 2025. This increase would become effective July 1, 2026. The ordinance approval is being requested at this time to provide sufficient time for the Des Moines Water Works to make the necessary changes to their billing program prior to the July 1 effective date. Previously, the ordinance also established a new rate category for flow to be received from the City of Altoona in the future under the terms of an agreement recently approved by both Cities.       • Vote 4b. Second Reading of Ordinance #970 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 106 Solid Waste Collection Fees DeMoss/ Motion: to approve the Second Reading of Ordinance #970 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 106 Solid Waste Collection Fees. City Manager Ben Champ explained that during the preparation of the FY27 budget there was discussion about the need to increase the solid waste collection fees and the recycling fees to reflect increases set by the Metro Waste Authority Board (MWA). The MWA manages the private hauling contractor that operates in Pleasant Hill and the current contract provides for cost adjustments each year based on tipping fees and fuel costs. The MWA Board has also made a decision to no longer subsidize the recycling operations from other landfill revenues. The two ordinances proposed will increase fees for the solid waste collection and the recycling collection fees, to be effective July 1, 2026. Approving the two ordinances at this time will provide time for the City to pass along the new rates to Des Moines Water Works so they have sufficient time to change the billing amount for July 1. The proposed increase in the solid waste fees is 2.7% to the hauler fee, and 2.5% for operational support, which increases the 96-gallon garbage container from $12.52 per month to $13.17 per month and the 48-gallon garbage container from $11.59 per month to $12.24 per month, for a total of $.65 cent increase per month. The last increase in these rates was July 1, 2025. The proposed increase for the recycling fee is 3.1% to the hauler fee, and 2.5% for operational support, increasing the rate from $4.91 per month to $5.18 per month, for a total of $.27 cent increase per month.       • Vote 9g. Resolution #052626-13 – Resolution Providing for Notice of Hearing on Proposed Amendment to the Revitalization Plan for the Pleasant Hill Urban Revitalization Area DeMoss/ Motion: to approve Resolution #052626-13 – Resolution Providing for Notice of Hearing on Proposed Amendment to the Revitalization Plan for the Pleasant Hill Urban Revitalization Area. City Manager Ben Champ explained that the City of Pleasant Hill has an adopted Urban Revitalization Plan that has been in place for many years to support housing and growth in the City. The Plan designates the entire City as an Urban Revitalization Area under the provisions of Iowa Code Chapter 404. The designation provides for the City’s tax abatement program that provides a deferment in the imposition of property taxes on the incremental value of new residential and commercial projects and qualifying remodels and additions. An amendment is now being proposed as an urgent response to the recent passage of SF 2472, a bill establishing new property tax regulations in the State of Iowa. The bill makes fundamental changes to tax increment financing and tax abatement. The two tools are used by communities across the country and have been specifically used throughout the state and in Pleasant Hill to encourage and facilitate economic development and broadening of the local tax base. The new law will now prevent the valuation generated by both programs from counting as new construction valuation for the City’s general fund in future budget years. This creates a uniquely untenable situation where incentivizing economic development for the long-term health of the community will now penalize municipal operations and create upward pressure on tax levies. As all new development in the City is potentially eligible for the program, the need to amend the tax abatement program is pressing. The City has worked with bond counsel to create an amendment to the Urban Revitalization Plan to cease acceptance of applications effective June 12th. The Plan would remain in existence and could be amended again to accept applications should new legislation supporting these economic development tools be passed in a future session. This is a resolution prepared by bond counsel setting a public hearing date for the June 9th Council meeting to consider the plan amendment. Councilmember Birch asked if there has been any feedback from Developers.
