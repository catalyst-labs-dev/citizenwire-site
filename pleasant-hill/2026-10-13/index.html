      • Vote 4b. Resolution #101326-03 – Approve Contract for Engineering Services for the SE 80th Street Pump Station and Sewer Main Improvements Project Konrad/ Motion: to approve Resolution #101326-03 – Approve Contract for Engineering Services for the SE 80th Street Pump Station and Sewer Main Improvements Project. Russ Paul, Public Works Director, presented the contract. In February 2025, a wastewater flow analysis update for the City’s East Sanitary Sewer Trunk Main was completed. The results of the analysis were discussed at a Council work session in September of last year. These results showed that the sanitary sewer pump station at SE 80th Street is currently at firm capacity and is close to design capacity. In late June the City posted a Request for Qualifications for engineering firms to submit proposals for this project. In total the City received five submissions to this request. Following a review and discussion of each of the proposals submitted, City Staff recommends City Council approve moving forward with Bolton and Menk for the design of the SE 80th Street Pump Station and Sewer Main Improvement Project, as the submission by Bolton and Menk encompasses the overall goals for the project. City Staff have worked with Bolton and Menk to assemble a contract for design and bid services to encompass the SE 80th Street Pump Station and Sewer Main Improvement project. a property owner, PE from Bolten and Menk gave a brief background on the firm and discussed the proposal submitted. He stressed the complexity of the project and importance of timing. Councilmember Konrad commented on the size of the project and the importance of getting this done correctly the first time as it will impact residents across the City. Councilmember Murray asked about the timeline of the project, and when engineering would begin. Mr. Ferrier explained they first need to review the data of the facilities study to determine accuracy, which will be started right awayPassed 5–0 · Minutes

      • Vote 5c. Resolution #101326-04 – Approve Payment Application No. 12 and Change Order No. 7 – 2025 Storm Sewer Improvements Project Birch/ Motion: to approve Resolution #101326-04 – Approving Payment Application No. 12 and Change Order No. 7 – 2025 Storm Sewer Improvements Project. Russ Paul, Public Works Director, explained City Council has identified the need to utilize American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) and stormwater utility funding to make stormwater improvements throughout the community. City staff have worked with the City Engineer to generate a list of storm sewer intakes in need of maintenance work, along with a needed stormwater improvement on Ash Drive and Willow Drive. Snyder and Associates have developed plans and specifications for the improvements and RW Excavating was awarded the contract and work is currently underway. The City Engineer has reviewed and recommends approval of Payment Application No. 12 and Change Order No. 7 for the project for work completed through September 30, 2026. Change Order No. 7 looks at improving severe undermining of street panels on Bartholomew Drive, by adding subdrain and replacing the settling pavement. Change Order 7 also includes adjustments of quantities to as-built quantities of various items of the project that have been completedPassed 5–0 · Minutes

      • Vote 6d. Resolution #101326-05 – Approve Payment Application No. 5 and Change Order No. 6 – Shadyview Blvd and University Ave Improvements Murray/ Motion: to approve Resolution #101326-05 – Approving Payment Application No. 5 and Change Order No. 6 University Avenue and Shadyview Boulevard Intersection Improvement Project. Russ Paul, Public Works Director, presented the intersection improvements at University Avenue and Shadyview Boulevard including turn lane additions and extensions, pedestrian crossing connections, and signalization. The City Engineer has reviewed and recommended approval of Payment Application No. 5 for the project for work completed through October 2, 2026. Change Order No. 6 relates to the addition of using a hydro mulching product at select project locations with total quantities being determined once applied. An update was provided on the project informing the Council that MidAmerican Energy has been on-site to set meters for operable streetlights, the area has been re-seeded by the subcontractor and hydromulch placed in areas where accessible. Additional sidewalk work was also taking place. There had also been discussions with the Iowa Department of Transportation regarding the median work to determine where the improvement stops for paving to meet up with existing asphalt and concrete. Staff emphasized to the contractor the importance of progression on the project. Mayor Kurovski thanked staff for their work on the project considering the complexities between multiple agencies involved. Councilmember Murray gave appreciation to staff for taking the lead and getting the project back on track. He emphasized the last paragraph of the letter provided by Snyder and Associates regarding working days and are now into liquidated damages considering the project had a 55 working day window. He asked that the paragraph be read into the record. City Manager Ben Champ read for the record the last paragraph of communication from the project engineer: We recommend payment of $152,338.50 to the contractor, All Star Concrete, LLC., for work completed through October 7, 2026. Approximately 74% of the total construction work has been completed. As of this payment period, 67 total working days have been charged. The contractor is currently twelve (12) working days beyond the 55 working days established by the contract. Liquidated damages in the amount of $1,000.00 a day are being tracked and will be assessed by change order upon completion of the projectPassed 5–0 · $152,338.50 · Minutes · Full page →
