      Approval of Final Plat for Elizabeth Gillotti Acres

      • Pleasant Hill City Council
      • May 12, 2026
      • Zoning
      What this is about

      Recorded in the minutes as: “g. Resolution #051226-07 - Approval of Final Plat for Elizabeth Gillotti Acres DeMoss/ Motion: to approve Resolution #051226-07 - Approval of Final Plat for Elizabeth Gillotti Acres. Senior Planner Brian Dunkelberger explained that the City has received the final plat for the Elizabeth Gillotti Acres residential subdivision located generally in the 5200 block of Pine Valley Drive and north of Evans Boulevard. This final plat covers approximately 5.26 acres of previously undeveloped land zoned as R-2: Single Family Attached Residential. The proposal is to subdivide the property into seven single-family residential lots, two outlots reserved for stormwater management, five outlots to be tied to adjacent neighboring properties, and one street lot extending Burr Oak Boulevard to connect Pine Valley Drive and Evans Boulevard. The preliminary plat for Elizabeth Gillotti Acres was approved by City Council on May 27, 2025”. Why this has a full page: zoning.

      What this means
      Plat
      A map that divides land into lots, streets and easements. A preliminary plat is the proposed layout. A final plat is the official version that gets recorded with the county.
      Resolution
      A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
