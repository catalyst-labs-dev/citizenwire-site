      • Vote 3a. First Reading of Ordinance #969 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 98 Sewer Service Charges Murray/ Motion: to approve the First Reading of Ordinance #969 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 98 Sewer Service Charges. City Manager Ben Champ explained that during the preparation of the FY27 budget it was identified that the rates for sanitary sewer service needed to be increased. The City of Pleasant Hill is a member of the Wastewater Reclamation Authority (WRA) and as a member must pay annually to the WRA for the City’s proportionate share of operation and maintenance costs and for debt service payments. The WRA has approved a new facility plan which calls for significant capital improvements and bonding in the upcoming fiscal years that will increase Pleasant Hill’s debt payments to the WRA. The rate increase proposed in this ordinance is 7.25% which will increase the base rate from $8.92 to $9.57 per 1,000 gallons and the sewer access charge from $14.96 to $16.05 per unit. That increase will impact the average residential customer by $1.74 per month. The increase will be .65 cents per 1,000 gallons of water consumed, plus an increase of $1.09 per month for the sewer access charge. The last rate increase the City approved was July 2025. This increase would become effective July 1, 2026. The ordinance approval is being requested at this time to provide sufficient time for the Des Moines Water Works to make the necessary changes to their billing program prior to the July 1 effective date. Previously, the ordinance also established a new rate category for flow to be received from the City of Altoona in the future under the terms of an agreement recently approved by both Cities. The agreement provides for a combined rate that would be adjusted at the same rates as other Pleasant Hill customers on July 1, 2026 from $12.47 to $13.38 per 1,000 gallons of flowPassed 4–0 · $16.05 · Minutes, p. 1 · Full page →
        What this means

        Ordinance readings: An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.

      • Vote 4b. First Reading of Ordinance #970 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 106 Solid Waste Collection Fees Murray/ Motion: to approve the First Reading of Ordinance #970 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 106 Solid Waste Collection Fees. City Manager Ben Champ explained that during the preparation of the FY27 budget there was discussion about the need to increase the solid waste collection fees and the recycling fees to reflect increases set by the Metro Waste Authority Board (MWA). The MWA manages the private hauling contractor that operates in Pleasant Hill and the current contract provides for cost adjustments each year based on tipping fees and fuel costs. The MWA Board has also made a decision to no longer subsidize the recycling operations from other landfill revenues. The two ordinances proposed will increase fees for the solid waste collection and the recycling collection fees, to be effective July 1, 2026. Approving the two ordinances at this time will provide time for the City to pass along the new rates to Des Moines Water Works so they have sufficient time to change the billing amount for July 1. The proposed increase in the solid waste fees is 2.7% to the hauler fee, and 2.5% for operational support, which increases the 96-gallon garbage container from $12.52 per month to $13.17 per month and the 48-gallon garbage container from $11.59 per month to $12.24 per month, for a total of $.65 cent increase per month. The last increase in these rates was July 1, 2025. The proposed increase for the recycling fee is 3.1% to the hauler fee, and 2.5% for operational support, increasing the rate from $4.91 per month to $5.18 per month, for a total of $.27 cent increase per month. The last increase in the recycling fees was July 1, 2025Passed 4–0 · $13.17 · Minutes, p. 2 · Full page →

      • Vote 13k. Resolution #051226-10 – Approve Revised Assigned Vehicle Use Policy DeMoss/ Motion: to approve Resolution #051226-10 – Approve Revised Assigned Vehicle Use Policy. City Manager Ben Champ explained that the City has a long-standing take-home car policy for key employees. The purpose of the policy is to provide for cost effective and efficient use of City resources while providing for the immediate needs of the City during an emergency. This is an update to the existing policy to add clarity and tax exemption for public safety employees who are required to take a vehicle home. Specifically, this is to address a current situation where it is to the City’s benefit for detectives and other on call public safety employees who are not the Chief to be assigned or required to use a take-home vehicle to facilitate response time. This is an existing practice, but some public safety employees are receiving a financial penalty for the taxable value of the commute without the policy updatesPassed 3–0 · Abstained: Konrad · Minutes, p. 4 · Full page →
