      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “e. Resolution #051226-05 – Approval of Salaries for Non-Union City Staff Salary Matrix for FY27 Hodges/DeMoss moved to approve Resolution #051226-05 – Approval of Salaries for Non- Union City Staff Salary Matrix for FY27. Motion: a 3.15% adjustment to the salary matrix for all non-union employees. The second resolution provides for a 3.15% adjustment to positions within the Fire Department that are not included in the salary matrix as the part-time and paid-on-call staff wages are built up based on certifications achieved”. The minutes add: “City Manager Ben Champ explained that the fiscal year 2026-27 will be the City’s tenth year using a matrix system for non-union employees. Prior to the adoption of the non-union salary matrix for the 2017-18 fiscal year, the City Council would approve a salary resolution every year prior to the beginning of each fiscal year for non-union employees to authorize the City Clerk’s office to pay salaries based upon the amounts in the resolution. The union employees’ salaries …”

      Motion as recorded: “a 3.15% adjustment to the salary matrix for all non-union employees. The second resolution provides for a 3.15% adjustment to positions within the Fire Department that are not included in the salary matrix as the part-time and paid-on-call staff wages are built up based on certifications achieved”.
