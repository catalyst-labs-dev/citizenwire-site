      • Vote 3a. Resolution #062326-05 – Approval of Lease Agreement with Pleasant Hill Chamber of Commerce Murray/ Motion: to approve Resolution #062326-05 – Approval of Lease Agreement with Pleasant Hill Chamber of Commerce. City Manager Ben Champ explained the City of Pleasant Hill has provided office space to the Pleasant Hill Chamber of Commerce for many years. A separate office suite at the 5160 Maple Drive office building containing City Hall is utilized by the Chamber at no cost and the common areas of the suite are available to the City and the Chamber. In exchange, the City is credited with a President’s level membership to the Chamber. Councilmember Len Murray shared the importance for the City having a strong Chamber of Commerce and the benefits of being collocated for collaborationPassed 4–0 · Minutes, p. 1

        What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      • Vote 4b. Approve Appointments to Boards and Commissions DeMoss/ Motion: to approve appointments to Boards and Commissions. City Manager Ben Champ provided an update on recommended appointments for Park and Recreation Commission and Library Board. The Park and Recreation Commission appointments for recommendation are Amanda Barlow, term expiring June 30, 2029 and a private individual, term expiring June 30, 2029 both for first terms. The Library Board appointments for recommendation are reappointing Amanda Michael for a second term, expiring June 30, 2030, and a private individual for a third term, expiring June 30, 2030Passed 4–0 · Minutes, p. 2
      • Vote 5c. Resolution #062326-06 – Approve Acceptance of the Carbon Reduction Program (CRP) Project Murray/ Motion: to approve Resolution #062326-06 – Approve Acceptance of the Carbon Reduction Program (CRP) Project. Russ Paul, Public Works Director explained the transportation improvement project with grant funding through the Des Moines Area Metropolitan Planning Organization and the Carbon Reduction Program to make improvements to the University Avenue corridor aimed at reducing carbon emissions. Meredith Emory, Project Engineer with Olsson and Associates provided a presentation regarding the success of the signal improvement project with the implementation of updated signal controllers and new software system installation.         What this means: Resolution. A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
