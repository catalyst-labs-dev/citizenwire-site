      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “b. Resolution #082526-03 – Approval of Preliminary Plat, Final Plat, and Site Plan – Journey Credit Union Konrad/ Motion: to approve Resolution #082526-03 – Approval of Preliminary Plat, Final Plat, and Site Plan – Journey Credit Union. Journey Credit Union is the developer and has immediate plans for acquisition and construction pending city approvals. The property is located within a C-1 Neighborhood Commercial zoning district which expressly permits this type of land use. The site plan designs a single-story building in the middle of the lot with a drive lane and parking spaces wrapped around. The site plan meets or exceeds minimum requirements for the proposed use. The Planning and Zoning Commission reviewed and recommended approval of the project. Resident Ray Sears asked about the number of handicapped parking stalls being provided on the site”.
