      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “c. Resolution #082526-04 – Approval of Immanuel Plat 1 Preliminary Plat DeMoss/ Motion: to approve Resolution #082526-04 – Approval of Immanuel Plat 1 Preliminary Plat. Brian Dunkelberger, Senior Planner presented a preliminary plat application for approximately 23.82 acres of undeveloped land generally located south of Morning Star Court. Immanuel is the developer and has plans to pursue a phased project consisting of independent/senior multifamily residential units and a PACE Center for senior care. The preliminary plat proposes three buildable lots and an outlot to be reserved for future development. Public improvements include an extension of sanitary sewer main, a ten-foot-wide public trail along Morning Star Court, and a street lot to be dedicated to the City at the time of final platting. All other infrastructure will be private, including water lines, internal access drives, and storm sewer and stormwater management facilities. Resident Ray Sears inquired regarding the financing for the project”.
