      • Vote 4a. Second Reading of Ordinance #974 – Vacation of Street Right-of-Way – portion of NE 12th Avenue in the 5100 block Konrad/ Motion: to approve the Second Reading of Ordinance #974 – Vacation of Street Right-of-Way – portion of NE 12th Avenue in the 5100 block. Brian Dunkelberger, Senior Planner presented the City has received a request from Elder Corporation to vacate and dispose of a portion of public right-of-way in the 5100 block of NE 12th Avenue. The vacation of this street right-of-way would remove its status as public roadway and would allow for the separate process of disposition where the property would be considered for sale to adjacent owners. Transfer to adjacent owners is the only available option in this case as the property is not large enough to be buildable on its own. Both adjacent owners were notified prior to the public hearing that occurred at the previous meetingPassed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 2

      • Vote 6c. Resolution #082526-04 – Approval of Immanuel Plat 1 Preliminary Plat DeMoss/ Motion: to approve Resolution #082526-04 – Approval of Immanuel Plat 1 Preliminary Plat. Brian Dunkelberger, Senior Planner presented a preliminary plat application for approximately 23.82 acres of undeveloped land generally located south of Morning Star Court. Immanuel is the developer and has plans to pursue a phased project consisting of independent/senior multifamily residential units and a PACE Center for senior care. The preliminary plat proposes three buildable lots and an outlot to be reserved for future development. Public improvements include an extension of sanitary sewer main, a ten-foot-wide public trail along Morning Star Court, and a street lot to be dedicated to the City at the time of final platting. All other infrastructure will be private, including water lines, internal access drives, and storm sewer and stormwater management facilities.
