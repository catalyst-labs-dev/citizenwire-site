      • Vote 4to approve Resolution 2026-204 approving Amendment No. 1 to the Kentucky Ridge/North 6th Street Urban Renewal Plan in the City of Indianola, State of IowaPassed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 2

        What this means: Tax increment financing (TIF). Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.

      • Vote 5to approve Resolution 2026-205 approving Change Order Numbers 9 and 10 and Change Order Request Number 16 for the CDBG Facade Program ProjectPassed 5–0 · Minutes, p. 2

        What this means: Change order. An approved change to a construction contract the city already signed. It can raise or lower the price, or change the work or the schedule.

      • Vote 6to approve Resolution 2026-206 directing the acceptance of a proposal to purchase $3,895,000 General Obligation Capital Loan Notes, Series 2026B; and approving the form and authorizing execution of a note purchase agreementPassed 4–1 · No: Armstrong · $3,895,000 · Minutes, p. 2 · Full page →

        What this means: Bonds and notes. Borrowing. The city sells bonds or notes to raise money now for projects and repays it with interest over years. General obligation (GO) debt is repaid from property taxes.
