      What this means
      Bonds and notes
      Borrowing. The city sells bonds or notes to raise money now for projects and repays it with interest over years. General obligation (GO) debt is repaid from property taxes.
      Resolution
      A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.

      A general definition of this kind of action, not a statement about this item.
