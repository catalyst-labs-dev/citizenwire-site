      More from the record

      Item 8 of the consent agenda, recorded in the minutes as: “Approval of a private party Charitable Trust Grant Agreement DEPARTMENT REPORTS & OTHER PRESENTATIONS Mayor Carver swore in an applicant as Fire/EMS Lieutenant. Adam’s family pinned on his badge. Chief Koch gave the police department quarterly report including several anniversaries, upcoming camera upgrade, and that the PD building generator was having the motor replaced under warranty. Sauter asked about volunteer opportunities for high schoolers for the department. Beck asked about hiring application numbers. Carter asked about the hiring timeline”. The council approved the whole consent agenda in one vote.

      The minutes give only the count (5–0), so individual votes are Not in the record. Vote line as printed: “Motion carried unanimously on a roll call vote 5-0.”.
