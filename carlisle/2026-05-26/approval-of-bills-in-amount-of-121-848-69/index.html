      Approval of Bills in Amount of $121,848.69

      Carlisle City Council · May 26, 2026 · City government

      Recorded in the minutes as “Approval of Bills in Amount of $121,848.69”. Passed 4–0 as part of the consent agenda; names not recorded. Amount in the record: $121,848.69.
