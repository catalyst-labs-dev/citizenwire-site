      Remove CIP quotes from table

      Carlisle City Council · May 11, 2026 · City government

      Recorded in the minutes as “Motion by Cataldo, second by back to remove CIP quotes from table. Carter noted concern that items were not in packet until Sunday”. Passed 4–1; Yes: Cataldo, Beck, Sauter, McAvoy. No: Carter.

      What this means (Capital improvement plan): A multi-year list of planned big projects, such as streets, buildings and equipment, with estimated costs.
