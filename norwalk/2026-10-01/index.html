        What this means: Bids and contracts. For public construction, the city approves plans and a cost estimate, takes bids, then awards the contract, usually to the lowest responsible bidder.

      • Vote 6to approve Resolution 26225 approving contract and bond for the Safe Routes to School Sidewalk ProjectCarried unanimously (roll call) · Count only · Minutes, p. 3

        What this means: Bonds and notes. Borrowing. The city sells bonds or notes to raise money now for projects and repays it with interest over years. General obligation (GO) debt is repaid from property taxes.

        What this means: Setting a hearing. This kind of vote schedules a public hearing for a later meeting and allows notice to be published. It does not approve the proposal itself.
