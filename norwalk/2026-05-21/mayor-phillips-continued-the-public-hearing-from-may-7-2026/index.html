      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “3. Mayor Phillips continued the public hearing from May 7, 2026 at 7:36 p.m. Luke Parris presented the amendment. Motion: to pass the First Reading of an Ordinance amending the Official Zoning Map of the City of Norwalk, Iowa by adding “Car Wash” as an additional Allowed Use in Land Use Parcel 3 of the Farms of Holland Planned Unit Development with a buffer requirement”. The minutes add: “Kaylen Petullo reported receiving written comments from Ryan Jensen at 3000 Sunset Dr. Norwalk, IA 50211. Jensen was also on Zoom to speak in favor of allowing a car wash. Mayor Phillips closed the public hearing at 8:05 p.m.”

      Motion as recorded: “to pass the First Reading of an Ordinance amending the Official Zoning Map of the City of Norwalk, Iowa by adding “Car Wash” as an additional Allowed Use in Land Use Parcel 3 of the Farms of Holland Planned Unit Development with a buffer requirement”.
