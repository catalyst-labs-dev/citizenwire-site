      Recorded in the minutes as: “Amendment. Lindsey Offenburger reported receiving no oral or written: to pass the first Reading of an Ordinance amending the Official Zoning Map of the City of Norwalk, Iowa by changing the Bulk Regulations for Accessory Structures in RE-1 Single Family Rural Estates, R-1 Single Family Residential, and R-F Founders Residential Zoning Districts, and amending Zoning Ordinance Chapter 175A.29.2 Accessory Buildings in Residential Zoning Districts”.

      Motion as recorded: “Amendment.
