      What this means
      Pay application
      A contractor’s request to be paid for work finished so far on an approved project. Cities often hold back a share, called retainage, until the job is complete.
      Resolution
      A formal council decision, usually made in one vote. It approves a specific action, such as a contract, plan or policy, without changing the city code.
