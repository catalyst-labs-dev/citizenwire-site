      More from the record

      Recorded in the minutes as: “B. Consideration of Resolution No. 2026-62 A Resolution Approving an Independent Administrative Services Agreement with Hart Halsey LLC DBA Extra Duty Solutions Police Chief Roth presented a proposed no-cost, month-to-month agreement with Extra Duty Solutions (EDS) to manage scheduling, billing, and Motion: Motion by Susan Skeries to APPROVE. Seconded by Joseph Jones.Roll call vote: Aye - Susan Skeries, Joseph Jones, Lauren Campbell, Fletcher Barry, Nay - Threase Harms”. The minutes add: “payment processing for extra-duty police assignments at Walmart. Roth explained that the current process requires significant officer time to coordinate daily assignments and fill last-minute vacancies, including with outside agencies. Costs associated with EDS would be paid by Walmart, with no cost to the City. Staff indicated the Walmart assignments would serve as a trial, with the potential …”

      Motion as recorded: “Motion by Susan Skeries to APPROVE. Seconded by Joseph Jones.Roll call vote: Aye - Susan Skeries, Joseph Jones, Lauren Campbell, Fletcher Barry, Nay - Threase Harms”.

      The minutes give only the count (4–1), so individual votes are Not in the record. Vote line as printed: “Motion Passed 4-1.”.
