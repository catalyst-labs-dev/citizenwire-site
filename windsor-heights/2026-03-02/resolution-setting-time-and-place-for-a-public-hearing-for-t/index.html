      What this means
      Setting a hearing
      This kind of vote schedules a public hearing for a later meeting and allows notice to be published. It does not approve the proposal itself.
      Budget
      The city’s spending plan for a fiscal year, which runs July 1 to June 30 in Iowa. Adopting it sets the property tax levy and needs a public hearing.

      A general definition of this kind of action, not a statement about this item.
