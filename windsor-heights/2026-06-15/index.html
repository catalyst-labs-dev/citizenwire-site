        Consent agenda: A list of routine items the council approves together in one vote, without separate discussion. Any member can ask to pull an item out for its own vote.

      • Vote 3A. Consideration of Tobacco and Alcohol Licenses for Pump & Pantry - 7220 Hickman Road Adam Plagge presented the item. Motion: APPROVEPassed 4–0 · Count only · Minutes
        What this means

        Alcohol and tobacco permits: Businesses need local approval before the state issues an alcohol or tobacco permit. Councils routinely sign off on new permits and renewals.

        Ordinance readings: An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.
