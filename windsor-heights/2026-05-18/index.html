        What this means

        Budget amendment: A change to the spending totals in the budget the city already adopted for the year. In Iowa it needs public notice and a public hearing first.

      • Vote 55. Consent Agenda: Any item on the Consent Agenda may be removed for separate consideration. Motion: APPROVEPassed 4–0 · Count only · Minutes
        What this means

        Consent agenda: A list of routine items the council approves together in one vote, without separate discussion. Any member can ask to pull an item out for its own vote.

      • Vote 6A. Consideration of the First Reading of Ordinance 26-01 - An Ordinance Adopting the "Code of Ordinances of the City of Windsor Heights, Iowa" a private individual presented the item and reviewed two proposed ordinance revisions following the public hearing. Motion: APPROVE Consideration of the First Reading of Ordinance 26-01 - An Ordinance Adopting the "Code of Ordinances of the City of Windsor Heights, Iowa" with the addition of the proposed staff changes and the update of peddler time restrictions in section 122.09Passed 4–0 · Count only · Minutes
        What this means

        Ordinance readings: An ordinance is a local law. In Iowa it is usually voted on at three separate meetings (first, second and third reading) before it passes. Each reading is one of those votes.
