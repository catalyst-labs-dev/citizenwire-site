      Privacy

      How Citizen Wire handles your email address when you sign up for alerts.

      Your email

      • We use your email only to send the alerts you choose.
      • We confirm your address before sending anything.
      • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
      • We do not sell or share your address.

      Services we use

      • We use EmailOctopus to send alerts.
      • Google reCAPTCHA blocks spam sign-ups (privacy, terms).

      What you can do

      • Pick the places and meetings you want alerts for.
      • Stop alerts any time with the unsubscribe link in any email.
      • Questions? Email alerts@citizenwire.org.
