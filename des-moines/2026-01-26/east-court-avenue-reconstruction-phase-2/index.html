      1 · What was decided

      The council approved a $4,198,776.71 contract to rebuild East Court Avenue from East 7th to East 14th Street with one lane each way and buffered bike lanes, by a 7–0 vote.

      • Roll call 26-0138
      • Jan 26, 2026
      • Getting around · City spending
      What this is about

      East Court Avenue from the Des Moines River to East 14th Street needs complete pavement reconstruction, curb ramps that meet ADA rules, and a new Des Moines Water Works main. Staff wrote that traffic has declined since Martin Luther King Jr. Parkway was built and several mixed-use developments have gone up nearby. Phase 1 (river to East 7th) was finished in fall 2024. This Phase 2 rebuilds the street to one travel lane each way with buffered bike lanes on both sides, plus sidewalks, storm sewer and water main. Four bids were received; Reilly Construction Co., Inc. (Ossian, Iowa) was lowest. Virtual public meetings were held in 2021 and 2022. Council Communication 26-019 (saved copy)

      The alternative

      No other options were presented in the record.

      The decision

      Approved plans and specifications and named Reilly Construction Co., Inc. the lowest responsive, responsible bidder at $4,198,776.71. Moved by Mandelbaum, seconded by Gatto.
