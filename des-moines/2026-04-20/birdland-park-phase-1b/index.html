      Des Moines › Apr 20, 2026 meeting › Item 42

      The council approved an $8,398,738.65 contract for Birdland Park Phase 1B, with a playground, skate ribbon and restroom building, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Apr 20, 2026 · Roll call 26-0528

      Approved plans and specifications and named Minturn, Inc. the lowest responsive, responsible bidder at $8,398,738.65. Moved by Westergaard, seconded by Simonson. A second roll call (26-0529) approved the contract and bond. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      More from the record

      Phase 1B is the second of three parts of Phase 1 of the Birdland Park and Marina Master Plan. It adds a concrete parking lot, open green space, a new playground, a skate ribbon, a restroom and concessions building, sidewalks and trail, and prairie and savanna plantings. The low bid of $8,398,738.65 was above the City Engineer’s $7,400,000 estimate; the funding list includes $800,000 of "anticipated value engineering." Council Member Gatto took part by phone.
