      Des Moines › Jun 29, 2026 meeting › Item 56

      The council changed the land-use plan and rezoned 3.24 acres near 1826 Hickman Road so Broadlawns Medical Center can build an employee parking lot, by a 6–1 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Jun 29, 2026 · Roll call 26-0777

      Approved the land-use plan change and rezoning, then passed the ordinance on first reading and waived the rest for final passage (roll calls 26-0778 and 26-0779), each 6–1. Moved by Simonson, seconded by Voss. Nays: Mandelbaum. Passed 6–1; voting no: Josh Mandelbaum.

      What this means (Rezoning): Zoning sets what can be built or done on each piece of land, such as homes, shops or industry.

      More from the record

      Broadlawns Medical Center asked to rezone several vacant parcels south of Hickman Road, across from the hospital, from "N5" and "NX1" neighborhood districts to Limited "P2" Public, Civic and Institutional, and to change the land-use plan from Low Density Residential to Public/Semi-Public. The lot would serve employees, freeing parking on campus for patients and visitors. Staff and the Plan and Zoning Commission (9–0 on May 21, 2026) recommended approval with two conditions: an enhanced pedestrian crossing on Hickman Road and driveway locations approved by the City Engineer. Resolution, staff report and P&Z record, roll call 26-0777 (saved copy)
