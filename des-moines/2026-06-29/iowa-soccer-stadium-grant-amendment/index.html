      Des Moines › Jun 29, 2026 meeting › Item 38

      The council approved a $656,074-a-year grant to the group building a pro soccer stadium on the city-owned Dico site, and set the schedule for a $7 million grant, by a 5–1 vote with one abstention.

      Des Moines City Council · Jun 29, 2026 · Roll call 26-0755

      Approved the first amendment to the grant agreement. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. Nays: Gatto (as the minutes record it). Mayor Boesen declared a conflict of interest and abstained. Passed 5–1; voting no: Joe Gatto.

      More from the record

      The Iowa Soccer Development Foundation (ISDF) is leasing part of the City’s 38-acre Dico Superfund site, south of MLK Parkway between SW 16th Street and the Raccoon River, to build Iowa’s first professional soccer stadium (at least 5,500 seats) and a public plaza by Dec 31, 2029. The lease approved June 8 sets rent at $656,075 a year for 25 years. This amendment gives ISDF a $656,074-a-year grant, paid from that rent, once the stadium and plaza are complete. It also schedules the earlier $7 million grant: $3 million at 75% completion or July 1, 2028; $2 million at 90%; $2 million at 100%. A completion bond is required. Council Communication 26-230 (saved copy)
