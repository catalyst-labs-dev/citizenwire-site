      Des Moines › Jun 29, 2026 meeting › Item 55

      The council rezoned 1011 Park Avenue to allow a 10-bed short-term shelter for adult women who survived human trafficking, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Jun 29, 2026 · Roll call 26-0774

      Approved the rezoning. Moved by Mandelbaum, seconded by Gatto. Ordinance readings followed. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      What this means (Rezoning): Zoning sets what can be built or done on each piece of land, such as homes, shops or industry.

      More from the record

      Youth Homes of Mid-America owns a former youth group home at 1011 Park Avenue. The applicant, Dorothy’s House, proposes using it as a 10-bed shelter for adult women survivors of trafficking, exploitation and prostitution, with stays of up to 30 days and 24-hour staff on site. The use needs "NX2" zoning; the site was "NX1." The shelter must meet supplemental rules, including floor space per occupant and a 1,320-foot spacing from similar facilities. Staff and the Plan and Zoning Commission (9–0 on May 21, 2026) recommended approval. Resolution and staff report, roll call 26-0774 (saved copy)
