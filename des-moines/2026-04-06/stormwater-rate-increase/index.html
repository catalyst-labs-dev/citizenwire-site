      Des Moines › Apr 6, 2026 meeting › Item 49

      The council raised the monthly stormwater charge 6%, from $19.29 to $20.45 per unit, starting July 1, 2026, by a 6–1 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Apr 6, 2026 · Roll call 26-0472

      Passed the ordinance on first vote (moved by Barron, seconded by Simonson), then waived the other two readings and gave it final passage (roll call 26-0473), each 6–1. Nays: Gatto. Passed 6–1; voting no: Joe Gatto.

      More from the record

      Every property in Des Moines except exempt property pays a monthly stormwater charge on the water bill, based on "equivalent residential units" (ERU) of 2,349 square feet of hard surface. The ordinance sets the rate at $20.45 per ERU from July 1, 2026, up from $19.29. The budget communication lists a 6% stormwater enterprise rate increase. By law the money can be used only for stormwater expenses. Ordinance in roll call 26-0472 (saved copy) · Council Communication 26-125 (saved copy)
