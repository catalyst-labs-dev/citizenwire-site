      Des Moines › Apr 6, 2026 meeting › Item 42

      The council denied rezoning 6.5 acres near 5600 SE 27th Street for up to 31 homes, by a 5–2 vote on a motion to deny.

      Des Moines City Council · Apr 6, 2026 · Roll call 26-0460

      Denied the rezoning. Moved by Gatto to deny, seconded by Voss. Nays (against denial): Barron and Mandelbaum. The ordinance readings that would have followed were withdrawn. Denied 5–2; voting no: Rob X. Barron, Josh Mandelbaum.

      What this means (Rezoning): Zoning sets what can be built or done on each piece of land, such as homes, shops or industry.

      More from the record

      C-BUILD, LLC asked to rezone an undeveloped 6.5-acre parcel from "N2b" to Limited "N2b-2" Neighborhood District to build up to 14 two-household dwellings (28 units) and three one-household homes. Neighbors to the north are single-family homes; to the south is an assisted living facility. City staff recommended approval and found it consistent with the Low Density Residential land-use plan. On March 5, 2026 the Plan and Zoning Commission voted 10–1–1 to recommend approval with conditions. The resolution notes approval needed six votes because more than 20% of nearby property owners objected. Resolution, staff report and P&Z record, roll call 26-0460 (saved copy)

      The alternative

      Approving the rezoning, as staff and the commission recommended, with conditions such as single-household homes west of a future SE 27th Street extension. Resolution 26-0460 (saved copy)
