      Des Moines › Apr 6, 2026 meeting › Item 44

      The council approved a $6,124,564.01 contract to rehabilitate the Guthrie Avenue bridge over the Union Pacific Railroad and add a 10-foot sidepath, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Apr 6, 2026 · Roll call 26-0465

      Approved plans and specifications and named Jasper Construction Services, Inc. the lowest responsive, responsible bidder at $6,124,564.01. Moved by Westergaard, seconded by Simonson. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      More from the record

      The project replaces the bridge deck overlay, expansion joints, barrier walls and medians, repaints the steel, makes structural repairs and adds a 10-foot multi-use sidepath on the south side with railings and fencing. It also rebuilds the Guthrie and Delaware Avenue intersection and replaces its traffic signals. Work will be staged to keep traffic moving each way. Submitted by the City Engineer. Council Communication 26-130 (saved copy)
