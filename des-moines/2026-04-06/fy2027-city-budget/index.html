      Des Moines › Apr 6, 2026 meeting › Item 47

      The council adopted the City’s $953,084,831 budget for the year starting July 1, 2026, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Apr 6, 2026 · Roll call 26-0469

      Adopted the FY2027 budget. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. The same meeting also amended the current-year budget (roll call 26-0468) and set a 6% stormwater rate increase (see the stormwater page). Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      What this means (Budget): The city’s spending plan for a fiscal year, which runs July 1 to June 30 in Iowa.

      More from the record

      This hearing adopted the recommended budget for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, across all City funds and including the capital budget. The Finance Director wrote that state limits on taxable value growth (HF 718) and rising costs make balancing the budget hard even at current service levels. To close the gap, the budget includes 16 position reductions (13 vacant, 3 changes in force) and changes to building security, the violence interruption program and library operations. New capital projects include an East University Avenue pedestrian bridge and Fire Station 12; three projects were removed. Self-supported improvement district rates stay the same. Four public meetings were held in December and January. Council Communication 26-125 (saved copy)
