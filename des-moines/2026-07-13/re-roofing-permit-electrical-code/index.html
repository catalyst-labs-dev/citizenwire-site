      Des Moines › Jul 13, 2026 meeting › Item 41

      The council will require a permit to re-roof homes and businesses starting Jan 1, 2027, and updated the electrical code, by a 6–1 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Jul 13, 2026 · Roll call 26-0851

      Passed on first vote (moved by Westergaard, seconded by Barron), then waived the other readings for final passage (roll call 26-0852), each 6–1. Nays: Gatto. Passed 6–1; voting no: Joe Gatto.

      More from the record

      The ordinance amends Chapter 26 of the City Code to require a re-roofing permit for both residential and commercial buildings beginning Jan 1, 2027, and to match recent state changes to the electrical code. Staff estimate $150,000 in permit fees from re-roofing projects. The Building and Fire Code Board of Appeals recommended the changes on June 11, 2026. Staff asked to waive the second and third readings, calling the changes editorial. Council Communication 26-244 (saved copy)
