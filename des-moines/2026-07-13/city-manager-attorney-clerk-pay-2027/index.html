      Des Moines › Jul 13, 2026 meeting › Item 33

      The council approved 3% raises for the City Manager, City Attorney and City Clerk, by a 5–2 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Jul 13, 2026 · Roll call 26-0842

      Approved the subcommittee’s recommendations. Moved by Simonson, seconded by Westergaard. Nays: Barron and Mandelbaum. Passed 5–2; voting no: Rob X. Barron, Josh Mandelbaum.

      More from the record

      The council’s personnel subcommittee (Boesen, Simonson, Voss) reviewed the three officials who report to the council. A Human Resources memo lists 3% base raises from FY2026, matching other supervisors and managers: City Manager Scott Sanders $337,563, City Attorney Jeff Lester $264,472, City Clerk Laura Baumgartner $151,528, effective June 22, 2026. The 6.5% retention bonus for the City Manager and City Attorney continues; the Clerk may cash out up to one week of vacation. Resolution and HR memo, roll call 26-0842 (saved copy)
