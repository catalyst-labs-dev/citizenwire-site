      Des Moines › Oct 5, 2026 meeting › Item 58

      The council approved a roundabout at East Army Post Road and Three Lakes Parkway and authorized getting the land, including by eminent domain, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Oct 5, 2026 · Roll call 26-1164

      Approved starting the project and authorized getting the needed property interests, including farmland, by gift, negotiation or eminent domain. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      More from the record

      A 2022 traffic study of four miles of East Army Post Road between US-69 (SE 14th St) and US-65 found speed was the main factor in injury crashes, and recommended roundabouts at several intersections. One at SE 36th Street was built in 2025. This project rebuilds the Three Lakes Parkway intersection as a single-lane roundabout. Iowa law requires a hearing before a city can use eminent domain on farmland; notices went to affected farmland owners on Sept 2, 2026. The city expects one partial land purchase and one temporary construction easement from one owner, with no relocation. Submitted by the City Engineer. Council Communication, item 58 (packet pages 895–897) (saved copy)
