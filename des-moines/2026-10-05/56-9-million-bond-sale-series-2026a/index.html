      Des Moines › Oct 5, 2026 meeting › Item 47

      The council set up the sale of $56,940,000 in general obligation bonds (Series 2026A), by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Oct 5, 2026 · Roll call 26-1147

      Directed advertisement for sale and approved the bidding procedures and Official Statement. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      What this means (Bonds and notes): Borrowing.

      More from the record

      Following the September hearings, this resolution combines the authorized general obligation bonds into a single $56,940,000 Series 2026A issue, approves electronic bidding through the Parity system and the preliminary Official Statement prepared with the City’s advisor, PFM Financial Advisors LLC. Bids are to be opened at 10:00 AM and acted on at a special council meeting on Oct 21, 2026 at 4:30 PM. It was part of the consent agenda; a companion item (roll call 26-1148) did the same for $6,400,000 in taxable Series 2026B bonds. Council packet, item 47 (saved copy)
