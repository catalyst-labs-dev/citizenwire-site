      Des Moines › Jun 8, 2026 meeting › Item 31

      The council approved preliminary terms for up to $850,000 in tax increment financing to renovate the former Office Max building at 2700 Ingersoll Avenue, by a 5–1 vote with one abstention.

      Des Moines City Council · Jun 8, 2026 · Roll call 26-0678

      Approved preliminary terms. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. Nays: Mandelbaum. Council Member Gatto declared a conflict of interest and abstained (as the minutes record it). Passed 5–1; voting no: Josh Mandelbaum.

      What this means (Tax increment financing (TIF)): Within an urban renewal area, the extra property tax from rising values can be set aside for a period of years to pay for projects or incentives in that area, instead of going to the usual taxing bodies.

      More from the record

      Ingersoll Properties Group, LLC plans an $8.2 million renovation of the 32,437-square-foot building that once held an Office Max and Dollar General, and its parking lot. UnityPoint’s PACE program for 55 to 65 older adults is slated for the west two-thirds; the rest is not leased. The developer agreed to make 50 parking spaces public for 15 years. The terms give TIF of up to $850,000 (net present value) instead of tax abatement, which staff wrote is less than the abatement the site could get by right. Value is expected to rise from $2,880,000 to about $7,314,000. Council Communication 26-212 (saved copy)
