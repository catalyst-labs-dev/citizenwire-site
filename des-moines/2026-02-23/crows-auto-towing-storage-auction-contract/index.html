      The council approved a three-year towing, storage and auction contract with Crow’s Auto Service, Inc., up to $300,000, by a 5–2 vote, after a motion to amend it failed 2–5.

      Approved the contract as presented. Moved by Westergaard, seconded by Gatto. Nays: Barron and Mandelbaum. Passed 5–2; voting no: Rob X. Barron, Josh Mandelbaum.

      More from the record

      The City asked for proposals on Oct 31, 2025 for towing and storing impounded vehicles and auctioning abandoned ones. A selection committee recommended Crow’s Auto Service, Inc. (826 21st Street), which has held the towing contract since 2015. The auction work had been done by another company that closed; staff wrote that combining it with Crow’s, where the vehicles are already stored, was most cost-effective. The contract runs March 1, 2026 to Feb 28, 2029, up to $300,000, with one optional one-year extension capped at a 5% increase. Submitted by the Chief of Police. Council Communication 26-072 (saved copy)

      The alternative

      Council Member Mandelbaum moved to approve the contract with amendments he submitted. That motion failed 2–5. What the amendments said: Not in the record. Minutes, item 35 (saved copy)
