      Des Moines › Oct 19, 2026 meeting › Item 28

      The council approved buying four replacement ambulances for $1,703,420, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Oct 19, 2026 · Roll call 26-1202

      Approved the purchase of four Type 1 ambulances from Foster Coach Sales, Inc. for $1,703,420. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      More from the record

      The Fire Chief asked to buy four Type 1 replacement ambulances, at $425,855 each, from Foster Coach Sales, Inc., the local vendor for REV Group (Horton Emergency Vehicles), through a competitively bid Sourcewell cooperative contract. Delivery is expected to take about three years, so the ambulances are being bought out of the FY2029 and FY2030 capital budgets. Power loads and power cots will be bought separately later. In March 2025 the council approved seven ambulances from the same vendor for $3,011,372. It was part of the consent agenda. Council Communication, item 28 (packet pages 327–328) (saved copy)
