      Des Moines › Aug 3, 2026 meeting › Item 61

      The council approved a $1,045,700 contract to repair the outside of 12 downtown skywalk bridges, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Aug 3, 2026 · Roll call 26-0931

      Approved plans, specifications and the engineer’s estimate, and named Minturn, Inc. (Brooklyn, Iowa) the lowest responsive, responsible bidder at $1,045,700: a $972,700 base bid plus $73,000 for the 5th Avenue bridge. A second roll call (26-0932) approved the contract and bond, 7–0. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      More from the record

      The City owns 52 skywalk bridges and is responsible for repairing them. After bid calls in 2022 and March 2026 drew zero bids, the City Engineer rebid the work on July 14, 2026. This first of three phases repairs exterior finishes, waterproofs joints and glazing seals, replaces lighting and cleans 12 skywalk bridges along Grand Avenue downtown. At the June 29 meeting the council asked for a separate price to keep the bridge over 5th Avenue (4E-5E) open, which was added as "Add-Alternate No. 1." The item was submitted by the City Engineer. Council Communication 26-270 (saved copy)

      The alternative

      A motion by Council Member Mandelbaum to approve the contract without the 5th Avenue bridge (Add-Alternate No. 1) failed 2–5. City staff also priced two other options for that bridge: demolition, estimated at $500,000 to $750,000, or permanently closing it off, estimated at $75,000 plus ongoing heating and cooling costs. If the work is not done, staff wrote, the bridges would keep degrading, with more emergency repairs and closures. Minutes, item 61 (saved copy) · Council Communication 26-270 (saved copy)

      Money

      Who
      City of Des Moines capital budget (Skywalk System, SW094)
      How
      $1,045,700 construction contract. The City Engineer had estimated $700,000 ($640,000 base plus $60,000 for the 5th Avenue bridge). Staff wrote that contractors cited elevated work, downtown traffic control and repeated moves around special events.
      When
      Contract approved Aug 3, 2026; funded from the 2026-27 Capital Improvement Program
      Source
      Council Communication 26-270 (saved copy)

      Time and getting around

      Who
      People using or passing under 12 skywalk bridges along Grand Avenue and nearby streets
      How
      Construction work over streets; staff note "significant traffic control in downtown." Specific closure dates: Not in the record.
      When
      Expected to start summer 2026 and finish spring 2027
      Source
      Council Communication 26-270 (saved copy)

      Health and safety

      Who
      Pedestrians at street level next to the skywalks
      How
      Recessed lighting replaced with LED lighting "to improve safety of adjacent street level pedestrians"; water damage repairs to seals and joints
      When
      Summer 2026 to spring 2027
      Source
      Council Communication 26-270 (saved copy)

      Who gains

      • Minturn, Inc.: receives the $1,045,700 construction contract. Minutes (saved copy)
      • Skywalk users: 12 bridges repaired, including the 5th Avenue bridge (4E-5E), which stays open. Staff note the Renaissance Savery Hotel has guest parking in the garage that connects to that bridge. CC 26-270 (saved copy)

      Who pays

      • City capital budget: $1,045,700, which is $345,700 above the engineer’s $700,000 estimate (calculated from the figures in the record). CC 26-270 (saved copy)
      • Downtown drivers and walkers: traffic control during construction. Length: Not in the record. CC 26-270 (saved copy)

      From the official minutes summary: all seven members were present (roll call 26-0866) and only Nays are named, so the rest voted yes.
