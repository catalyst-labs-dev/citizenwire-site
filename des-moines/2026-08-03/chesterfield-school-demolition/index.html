      1 · What was decided

      The council approved demolishing the former Chesterfield School at 2501 Maury Street without further historic review, by a 7–0 vote.

      • Roll call 26-0936
      • Aug 3, 2026
      • Historic buildings · Housing
      What this is about

      The former Chesterfield School, built around 1880, has been owned by the City since 1975 and has been vacant for an extended period. Joppa Village Project leases it with an option to buy and has been planning a tiny home community there. Joppa, through a representative, asked to tear the school down. City staff found the building meets the criteria for landmark designation, so the council had to decide whether to send the request to the Historic Preservation Commission for study. Joppa told the City that renovating would cost about $1.6 million more than demolishing and building new, and that the building would not meet its space needs (Joppa’s figures). Council Communication 26-259 (saved copy)

      The alternative

      Alternative A in the resolution: refer the request to the Historic Preservation Commission, which would have had 90 days to work with Joppa on options such as landmark designation, rehabilitation with tax incentives, a new use, a new owner or moving the building. Resolution 26-0936 (saved copy) · CC 26-259 (saved copy)

      The decision

      Approved the demolition request (Alternative B), subject to City Code § 58-70(d): Joppa must photograph and document the building and salvage as many fixtures and materials as possible before a demolition permit is issued. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss.
