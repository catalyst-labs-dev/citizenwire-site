      Des Moines › Sep 14, 2026 meeting › Item 49

      The council suspended the city-wide property tax abatement program at the end of 2026, by a 6–1 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Sep 14, 2026 · Roll call 26-1065

      Closed the hearing, overruled objections and adopted the Second Amendment to the Fourth Restated City-wide Urban Revitalization Plan. Moved by Mandelbaum, seconded by Barron. Nays: Westergaard. Passed 6–1; voting no: Linda Westergaard.

      What this means (Abatement): In city government this usually means one of two things: reducing property taxes on new construction for a set number of years, or requiring or arranging the cleanup of a nuisance or unsafe property.

      More from the record

      Des Moines has offered tax abatement city-wide since 1987, temporarily reducing property taxes on new improvement value. The Development Services Director wrote that state Senate File 2472 restricts how much General Fund revenue can grow when abated value returns to the tax rolls, so continuing abatement would hurt funding for services such as firefighters, police and parks. The amendment suspends all abatement at the end of 2026. Projects with permits issued and taxable value added by then can still get abatement under current schedules. It does not affect abatement properties already receive or applications already approved. Notice ran in the Des Moines Register on Sept 2, 2026.
