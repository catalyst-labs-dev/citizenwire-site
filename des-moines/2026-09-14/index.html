      What this means

      Budget: The city’s spending plan for a fiscal year, which runs July 1 to June 30 in Iowa. Adopting it sets the property tax levy and needs a public hearing.

      City Council

      Council advances $42M and $18M bond issues

      Sep 14, 2026 · Vote 7–0 · Roll call 26-1066

      What this means

      Bonds and notes: Borrowing. The city sells bonds or notes to raise money now for projects and repays it with interest over years. General obligation (GO) debt is repaid from property taxes.

      City Council

      Council suspends city-wide tax abatement after 2026

      Sep 14, 2026 · Vote 6–1 · Roll call 26-1065

      What this means

      Abatement: In city government this usually means one of two things: reducing property taxes on new construction for a set number of years, or requiring or arranging the cleanup of a nuisance or unsafe property. The item text says which.
