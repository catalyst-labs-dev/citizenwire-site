      Des Moines › Sep 14, 2026 meeting › Item 57

      The council approved the City Manager’s budget-cut package, including eliminating 43 positions (15 filled), by a 6–1 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · Sep 14, 2026 · Roll call 26-1080

      Approved the reduction in force and the City Manager’s recommendations on services and revenue. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. Nays: Westergaard. Passed 6–1; voting no: Linda Westergaard.

      What this means (Budget): The city’s spending plan for a fiscal year, which runs July 1 to June 30 in Iowa.

      More from the record

      The City Manager wrote that state law changes, mainly a property tax growth cap, leave an estimated $12 million budget gap for FY2028 and another $5 million for FY2029. His first package, presented Aug 26, 2026, cuts $5,490,000 in spending and includes revenue changes. It eliminates 43 positions: 28 vacant and 15 filled. The filled positions are in the City Manager’s Office (1), Neighborhood Services (2), Parks and Recreation (3 park and cemetery workers), Community Impact and Relations (3, including the director and a human rights specialist), Development Services (2 inspectors) and IT (4). Affected employees were offered separation packages; layoffs would take effect no later than November 2026. Council packet, item 57 (saved copy)

      The alternative

      No other options were presented in the record. Details of the revenue recommendations: Not in the record.
