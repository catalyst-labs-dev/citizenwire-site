      Des Moines › May 4, 2026 meeting › Item 25(C)

      The council backed a $1,000,000 state workforce housing tax credit application for Mezzo on Walnut, a $77 million, 209-unit building at 606 Walnut Street, by a 7–0 vote.

      Des Moines City Council · May 4, 2026 · Roll call 26-0566

      Approved the resolution of support. Moved by Gatto, seconded by Voss. Passed 7–0; no members voted no.

      More from the record

      Lawmark, LP plans a mixed-use residential building at 606 Walnut Street, downtown, with about 209 efficiency apartments. In January 2025 the council approved amended terms of a development agreement for the project, estimated at $77,000,000, under which the City provides $10,000,000 in tax increment financing incentives. The state Workforce Housing Tax Credit program requires a city resolution of support; this one supports an application for $1,000,000 in credits. It was part of the consent agenda. Resolution, roll call 26-0566 (saved copy)
