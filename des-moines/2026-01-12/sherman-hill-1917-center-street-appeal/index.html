      More from the record

      Colbert Properties proposed a two-story house and a detached four-car tandem garage on a vacant lot at 1917 Center Street in the Sherman Hill Local Historic District. The house is described as a modern take on the International Style with Art Deco influences: flat roof, stucco and metal-clad windows. The Historic Preservation Commission (HPC) conditionally approved it on Aug 20, 2025. The Sherman Hill Association appealed, and on Oct 20, 2025 the council sent it back to the HPC, which approved it again with added conditions on Nov 5, 2025. The adjacent property owner at 1921 Center Street then appealed, arguing the decision was arbitrary and did not apply the city’s historic-district design guidelines. City staff wrote that the design is compatible with the district. Council Communication 26-001 (saved copy)

      The alternative

      The council could affirm, reverse, or send the decision back to the HPC again. The appellant asked the council to review the project on its merits or remand it for a rehearing. Council Communication 26-001 (saved copy)
